## Overview

Guest payments go straight into your own Stripe account. OpenBookings never holds your money.

## The timeline

1. The guest pays when they book. The payment lands in your Stripe balance, minus Stripe's processing fee and the OpenBookings commission.
2. Stripe pays your balance out to your bank account on the payout schedule set in your Stripe Dashboard.

## Changing when you are paid

The payout schedule, and the bank account payouts go to, are settings in your Stripe Dashboard. Sign in at [dashboard.stripe.com](https://dashboard.stripe.com) to see upcoming payouts or change either.

New Stripe accounts can have a longer delay on their first payouts; Stripe shows the expected arrival date for each one.
