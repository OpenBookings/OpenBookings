*Last updated: 27 November 2026*

## Overview

**Reviews** sits under Bookings in the sidebar. It lists the reviews guests wrote about your properties, and the number next to it shows how many are waiting for a reply.

Only guests who booked through OpenBookings and stayed can review. We email them a day after check-out. A review shows a score from 1 to 10, the guest's first name and country, and the month of the stay, never the exact dates.

## The page

- **The summary** shows the average guests see on your property page (from 3 reviews, over the last 24 months), how the scores spread over Terrific, Great, Good, Okay and Bad, and how many reviews wait for a reply.
- **To reply** lists the reviews you haven't handled yet. **All** lists every published review. **Being checked** lists reviews our automatic check held back. A person at OpenBookings decides on them (see [What we check](#what-we-check)).
- Click a review to read it. **View reservation** opens the booking it belongs to.

## Replying

Every reply starts with one question: **publicly** or **privately**?

- **Publicly** posts your reply under the review on your property page, signed with the property name. Your own name is never shown. The guest gets an email with your reply and can answer it once, publicly. You then get the last word, after which the thread closes and you carry on in Messages.
- **Privately** sends a message to the guest in the booking's conversation in [Messages](/business/messages), with the review attached. Nothing is shown on your property page.
- **No reply needed** takes the review off **To reply** without sending anything. You can undo it, or still reply later. If the guest answers you after that, the review needs a reply again.

Replies go through the same check as reviews, and are held the same way.

You can edit your public replies at any time; an edited reply shows "Edited". You can delete your latest reply in a thread. You can't edit or remove a guest's review. If you think a review breaks the rules, report it (see [Reporting a review](#reporting-a-review)).

## What we check

Every review, every reply of yours and every answer from a guest follows our [review rules](https://openbookings.co/legal/review-rules), and is checked when it's posted:

1. An automatic check looks for contact details and links.
2. A second automatic check, by a provider based in the EU, reads the text against the rules. It gets the text only: never the score, your property, the guest or the booking.

If nothing seems to break a rule, the text is published straight away. If something does, or the check can't run, the text waits in **Being checked** until a person at OpenBookings decides.

The score never counts. A low score is never a reason to hold or remove a review.

If nobody has decided within 72 hours, the text is published automatically, with the parts that seem to break a rule replaced by "[removed]". A person can still remove it after that.

## Decisions

A person at OpenBookings either keeps the text as written or removes it, naming the rule it broke. Nobody edits anyone's words, yours included. We never remove a review for its score or because it's negative: only for breaking the rules, or where the law requires it.

If we remove one of your replies, or it's published at the deadline with parts replaced, we send you a short email. Open the review in **Reviews** to see what happened and why: the rule or rules involved, which parts were replaced, any note from the person who decided, and whether it was decided automatically. If you disagree, reply to the email or contact support. We don't email you when a reply is kept.

A guest whose review was removed may write it once more while reviews for that stay are still open. If that one is removed too, they can't write another.

You can't approve, hold or remove reviews.

## Reporting a review

Open the review, click **Report…**, pick the rule it breaks and, if you like, add a note. The review stays visible while a person checks it and shows **Reported · being checked**. We email you the outcome, and it shows on the review in **Reviews**: **Report upheld: removed** (the review is taken down; it stays in **All** so you can see what happened) or **Report not upheld** (it stays up). A low score alone isn't a reason to report. If a review you reported was kept and something new comes up, you can report it again. A guest's answer to your reply can be reported the same way, with **Report…** on the answer.

Anyone can also report a review from your property page. People who are signed in can ask for the outcome by email.

Reports, and the text of anything we removed, are deleted 12 months after the decision. A report nobody decided on is deleted 12 months after it was made.
