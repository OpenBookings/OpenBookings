## Overview

The business portal provides access to all your property's settings, rates, and reservations. 

## Permissions 

There are no distinct roles — such as 'admin' or 'receptionist' — within a business account. Every member who signs in shares full access. If someone can access your property's dashboard, they can close rooms, change availability, and view financial data.

## Limitations and warnings

- You cannot restrict certain staff to a read-only view.
