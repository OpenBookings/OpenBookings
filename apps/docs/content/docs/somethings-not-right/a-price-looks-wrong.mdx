## Overview

If a guest booked a price that seems lower or higher than you expected, an adjustment has fired on top of the base rate.

## How to check price adjustments

1. Go to **Rates & Availability**.
2. Select the specific date cell for the room type and rate plan the guest booked.
3. Review the **Price breakdown** panel.
4. It lists the base rate and all modifiers in the order they were applied. Look for discounts that apply to longer stays (marked with an `*` on the grid) or day-of-week uplifts.

## Limitations and warnings

- You cannot remove or edit price adjustments yourself.
