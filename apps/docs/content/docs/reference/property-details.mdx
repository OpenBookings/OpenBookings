## Overview

**Property** under the Property section is where your property's own details will live — its name, description, photos and house rules. It is not built yet: opening it shows a **Coming soon** message.

## What you can do here

Nothing yet. The page is a placeholder.

## Where these details come from today

The property name, tagline, description, photos and house rules you entered during setup are the ones in use. See [Setting up your property](/docs/tutorials/setting-up).

## Limitations and warnings

<Callout type="warn">
  Because setup can only be completed once, there is currently no way to change your property's name, description, photos, house rules or address yourself. Contact support if any of them need correcting.
</Callout>
