## Overview

Your account details sit behind your name and email at the bottom of the sidebar. Selecting it opens a short menu with **Profile**, **Security** and **Log out**.

## What you can do here

- See the name and email your account is signed in with
- Log out
- Open the Profile and Security pages, which are not yet built

## Who can do what

There are no separate roles in the dashboard today. Everyone who signs in to your property's business account sees the same screens and can take the same actions — there is no reception, manager or administrator distinction, and no way to give someone limited access.

<Callout type="warn">
  Because access is all-or-nothing, anyone you give sign-in access to can change your rates, close dates and take rooms off sale. Share access carefully.
</Callout>

Each business account works with the property it owns. You will not see other properties' bookings, messages, rates or availability, and they will not see yours.

## How to log out

1. Select your name and email at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Choose **Log out**.

You are returned to the sign-in page.

## Profile and Security

Both pages exist in the menu but show a **Coming soon** message.

- **Profile** will hold your personal details.
- **Security** will hold sign-in and security settings.

## Limitations and warnings

- You cannot change your name, email or photo. The picture beside your name is generated automatically from your name.
- There are no security settings to configure — sign-in is by email link, Google or Microsoft, and there is no password to change.
- There is no way to invite a colleague, remove someone's access, or see who else has signed in.
