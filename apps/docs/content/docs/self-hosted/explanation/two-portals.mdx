---
title: Two portals, two sessions
description: Why guests and hotel staff sign in to separate apps with separate accounts.
---

OpenBookings has two front doors: the guest site and the host dashboard. They run as separate
apps, on separate host names, with separate sign-in sessions, even though they share one database
and one `user` table.

## Accounts belong to one portal

Every user has an `account_type`: `private` for guests, `business` for hotel staff. A guest
account can't sign in to the host dashboard, and a hotel account can't sign in to the guest site.
A missing type is refused, never guessed.

This keeps two kinds of risk apart. A guest session can only see that guest's own bookings. A host
session can change prices, refund payments and move payouts, so it carries extra protection:
passkeys, two-factor and a fresh check ("step-up") before sensitive actions.

## Cookies that can't mix

Each app runs its own Better Auth instance with its own secret and its own cookie prefix
(`ob-guest` and `ob-host`). Even on the same parent domain, one app's session cookie means
nothing to the other.

That's also why local development uses `web.localhost` and `business.localhost` rather than two
ports on `localhost`. Browsers share cookies across ports on the same host name, and two names
keep the sessions apart the same way they are in production.

## Organisations and roles

Hotel staff work inside an **organisation**, which owns properties. A member's role decides what
they can do, and on which properties:

| Role | Shown as | Can | Properties |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `owner` | Owner | everything a general manager can, plus closing the account | all |
| `admin` | General manager | bookings, refunds, rates, listings and the team | all |
| `manager` | Manager | listings, rates and bookings; no refunds beyond the policy, no team or payouts | chosen |
| `reservations` | Reservations | bookings, check-ins and cancellations; sees rates but can't change them | chosen |
| `frontdesk` | Front desk | sees bookings, checks guests in, marks no-shows and early departures | chosen |
| `finance` | Back office | bookings, rates, payout status, commission invoices and billing details | all |

"Chosen" properties are the ones the member has been given access to. The rules live in
`@openbookings/authz` (`role-guide.ts` and `permissions.ts`), and every request on the host
dashboard goes through it.
