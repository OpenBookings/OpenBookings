---
title: Limits
description: What the hard boundaries are for sizes, lengths, and retentions.
---

*Last updated: 10 September 2026*

- **Room photos** — The maximum file size is exactly 10 MB (10,485,760 bytes) per photo. Files larger than this are rejected.
- **Messages** — A single message body cannot exceed 5,000 characters.
- **Message retention** — Conversations with no activity for 365 days are cleared automatically and the sender is anonymised. This cannot be undone.
- **Support bot context** — The chat context you have with the automated support bot is cached for exactly 6 hours.
- **Status cache** — System status and updates are cached for 15 minutes.
- **Cookie consent** — Cookie consent choices expire after 90 days.
- **Commission notice** — Any change to your commission rate requires 30 days' notice.
- **Room holds** — A checkout session holds a room for 30 minutes.
