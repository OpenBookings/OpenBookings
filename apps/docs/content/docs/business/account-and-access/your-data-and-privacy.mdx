## Overview

OpenBookings collects and processes data solely to facilitate bookings and property management.

## Cookie consent

Your analytics and cookie consent choices expire automatically after 90 days. You will be prompted to choose again once that time elapses.

## Data retention

Specific retention periods apply for system data. For example, messages are kept for 365 days before automatic anonymisation. If you need a copy of guest communication, you must secure it before that window closes.
