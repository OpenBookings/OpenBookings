## Overview

OpenBookings charges a commission on each successfully completed booking. It covers the guest discovery platform and business tooling. Payment processing is passed through directly at cost.

## The calculation

Commission is a flat percentage taken from the booking subtotal (the room rate). It is not charged on tourist tax or the payment processing fees. 

The commission is taken before the payout is sent to you; you do not receive a separate bill to settle.

## Limitations and warnings

<Callout type="warn">
  There is currently a discrepancy between systems: the platform defaults to a commission rate of 3.5%, while the marketing copy and Cost Calculator state a commission of 4.5%.
</Callout>

- Any changes to the commission rate you agree with OpenBookings strictly require 30 days' notice before taking effect.
