<Section id="what-we-collect" number="2" title="What we collect and why">

We only collect data we actually need. Here's the full list.

<CardGroup>

<DataCard title="2.1 Account data">

Your email address and name. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we also receive the name and profile picture that provider shares with us.

If you add them in your personal details: your phone number, your address and your country. The property you booked with can see your phone number and address, so it can reach you about your stay; every time someone there opens them, we record who and when. Your country is shown with any review you write.

To keep your account secure: your passkeys (the name you gave each one, its device type and when you added it) and your sign-in sessions (when you signed in, the IP address and the browser it came from).

The rooms you like, so you can find them again.

<Meta why="You need an account to use the platform, and to keep it secure." basis="Performance of a contract." />

</DataCard>

<DataCard title="2.2 Booking data">

Guest name, contact details, and booking details: stay dates, property, number of guests, payment reference, and status.

Card details never reach our servers. Stripe handles those; we only receive the payment reference.

We also use your booking, without your name or contact details, to give the property statistics about its own bookings, such as revenue, occupancy and where its guests come from. Groups smaller than five guests are never shown. Details about who you are (your country and party size) are used for this for 24 months after you book.

The property you book with receives your booking details and uses them as a separate controller, under its own privacy policy.

<Meta why="To process your booking, handle anything that comes up around it, and give the property statistics about its bookings." basis="Performance of a contract; legal obligation for financial records; legitimate interest in giving properties insight into their own bookings." />

</DataCard>

<DataCard title="2.3 Analytics data">

Page views, navigation paths, approximate device type, browser, and country.

Via PostHog. We never pass your name, full IP address, or payment details into analytics events.

If you're signed in and accepted analytics cookies, events are linked to your account so we can understand usage across sessions and devices. The link is your account's internal ID — a random identifier, not your name or email.

If you accepted analytics, we also see whether you opened the emails we send you and which of their links you clicked, linked to the same account ID.

<Meta why="To understand usage and fix issues." basis="Consent, via the cookie banner. No acceptance, nothing tracked." />

<Callout title="Session replay">

We record on-screen interactions to spot usability issues in the booking flow.

<DashList items={[
  "All text is masked. We never see what you type into forms.",
  "Recordings are deleted automatically after 30 days.",
  "Only runs if you accept analytics cookies.",
]} />

</Callout>

</DataCard>

<DataCard title="2.4 Error data">

When something breaks, Sentry receives the error, a stack trace, the page you were on, and your browser type. Sentry also receives timing data for the pages you load, so we can see when the site is slow. No form contents, no payment details.

<Meta why="To find and fix bugs." basis="Legitimate interest in keeping the platform working." />

</DataCard>

<DataCard title="2.5 Transactional emails">

Your email address and the details needed for the message: booking confirmations, check-in details, receipts, and sign-in links.

<Meta basis="Performance of a contract." />

_No marketing emails. Every email is triggered by something you did, or by a stay you booked (see 2.7)._

</DataCard>

<DataCard title="2.6 Messages">

The messages you and a property send each other through OpenBookings, with the time they were sent and read. Messages are checked automatically for scams, phishing and requests to pay outside OpenBookings, as our Terms of Service explain.

<Meta why="So you and the property can arrange your stay." basis="Performance of a contract; legitimate interest in keeping you safe from fraud." />

</DataCard>

<DataCard title="2.7 Reviews">

After a stay, we email you an invitation to review it, and one reminder six days later if you haven't. You can turn these emails off with the link in each one.

If you write a review, we keep your score, your text and any answer you post under the property's reply. Your review is public on the property's page, with your first name, your country, the month of your stay and the room you stayed in. Reviews that contain contact details or links are held until our team has checked them.

<Meta why="So future guests can read about real stays." basis="Legitimate interest in giving guests honest information about properties, for the invitation emails; your choice to publish, for the review itself." />

</DataCard>

<DataCard title="2.8 Support conversations">

When you email our support team, we keep the conversation and your email address. Replies may be drafted with help from our AI assistant, and you can ask for a person at any time. Once the assistant has verified that the email comes from you, it can look up your bookings to help with the reply.

<Meta why="To answer your question." basis="Performance of a contract." />

</DataCard>

<Section id="cookies" number="3" title="Cookies">

When you first visit, you'll see a cookie banner. Your choices are simple:

<ChoiceList>
  <Choice label="Accept">PostHog starts, collecting usage data and session recordings.</Choice>
  <Choice label="Decline">Nothing analytics-related gets set. PostHog doesn't run at all.</Choice>
</ChoiceList>

<Note>EU/EEA visitors see this banner until they make a choice. Changed your mind? Open Help and choose Cookie Settings to decide again.</Note>

**What we actually set:**

| Type | Name | What it does | Expires | Needs consent? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Essential | `session token` | Keeps you signed in | Session / configurable | No |
| Essential | `cf_clearance` | Cloudflare bot protection | 30 minutes | No |
| Essential | `ob_cookie_consent` | Remembers your consent choice (local storage, our own banner). The choice is also recorded on our servers as proof, without your IP address, and linked to your account if you are signed in | 90 days | No |
| Essential | `ob_consent_outbox`, `ob_consent_linked` | Delivers that record to our servers and remembers it was linked to your account (local storage) | Until delivered / until you clear site data | No |
| Analytics | `ph_*` | PostHog analytics and session replay | 1 year | Yes, EU/EEA |

<Note>No advertising cookies. No social media cookies.</Note>

**Emails.** If you accepted analytics, the booking confirmations, review emails and support replies we send you contain a tiny invisible image that tells us when the email is opened, and our links in them go through us first so we can see which one you clicked. Without your consent we send these emails without that image and with plain links.

</Section>

<Section id="retention" number="4" title="How long we keep things">

We don't hold onto data longer than we need to.

| Data | Kept for | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Account data | Until you delete your account | So you can keep using your account |
| Messages | 365 days after the last message in the conversation, then the text is removed | Settling questions about a stay |
| Notes you add to a booking, and cancellation reasons | 18 months after check-out | Settling questions about a stay |
| Reviews | Until you delete the review or your account. After you delete your account, the review stays without your name or country | Honest information for future guests |
| Booking records | 7 years from booking date | Dutch tax and financial records law |
| Analytics data | 13 months (rolling) | Year-over-year trend analysis |
| Session recordings | 30 days | Usability analysis only |
| Error reports | 90 days | Debugging |
| Email logs | 90 days | Debugging delivery issues |
| Consent records | 5 years | Proof of the cookie choice you made |

<Note>After these windows close (for account data: when you delete your account), data is deleted or anonymised. Anonymised data may stick around for statistics.</Note>

</Section>

<Section id="security" number="6" title="Security">

Here's what we do to keep your data safe:

- Everything travels over encrypted connections (TLS).
- Secrets live in a Secret Manager, not in code.
- Infrastructure is hosted in the EU on Scaleway, with access controls and audit logging.
- Card details never touch our servers. Stripe handles all of that.
- Session recordings are masked, so we never capture sensitive inputs.

<Note>No system is completely bulletproof. If we ever find out your data was compromised, we'll tell you and the relevant authority as required by law.</Note>

</Section>

<Section id="your-rights" number="7" title="Your rights under GDPR">

| Right | What it means for you |
| --- | --- |
| **Access** | Get a copy of everything we hold about you. |
