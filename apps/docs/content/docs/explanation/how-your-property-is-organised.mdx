## Overview

Everything in the dashboard hangs off three layers. Getting them straight explains most of what you will see later — particularly why one change ripples further than you expected.

## The three layers

### Your property

The top level. One business account manages one property. Its name, description, photos, house rules and address are set once during setup.

### Room types

A room type is a group of interchangeable rooms — *Deluxe Double*, *Family Room*. What matters is the **number of units** it has: how many physical rooms of that kind you can sell.

Availability is counted per room type, not per individual room. If *Deluxe Double* has eight units and three are booked, five are available. The dashboard does not track which specific room a guest is in.

### Rate plans

A rate plan is one *way of selling* a room type. The same rooms might be sold as a flexible rate and as a cheaper non-refundable rate. Each plan carries its own:

- Base rate
- Refundable or non-refundable terms, and a cancellation policy
- Minimum and maximum stay
- Price adjustments

## The one rule to remember

> **Rate plans on the same room type all draw from the same pool of rooms.**

Adding a rate plan gives guests another way to book. It does not give you another room to sell.

This single fact explains behaviour that otherwise looks like a bug:

| What you see | Why |
| --- | --- |
| A room type sells out, and *every* one of its rate plans goes unsellable at once | They share one pool, and the pool is empty. |
| Closing one rate plan leaves the others still selling | Closures apply to a plan, not to the rooms. |
| Blocking rooms affects every plan on that room type | Blocking removes rooms from the shared pool. |

So when you want something off sale, the question is always: **the rooms, or one way of selling them?**

- Rooms unavailable — a burst pipe, an owner staying — change [availability](/docs/explanation/availability).
- One offer withdrawn — pulling the non-refundable rate over a busy weekend — set a [closure](/docs/explanation/restrictions-and-closures).

## How this shows up in the grid

In [Rates & Availability](/docs/reference/rates-and-availability), a collapsed room type is one row showing rooms free per date. Expanding it reveals an **Available rooms** row — the shared pool — with the rate plan rows beneath it. The layout is the hierarchy: the pool on top, the ways of selling it below.

## Tips

- When something looks wrong on a rate plan, check the **Available rooms** row above it first. Half the time the answer is there.

## Limitations and warnings

- Room types and their unit counts cannot be created or edited from the dashboard yet. See [Rooms](/docs/reference/rooms).
- Each business account works with the property it owns. You will not see other properties, and they will not see yours.
