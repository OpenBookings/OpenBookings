## Overview

Connecting takes a few minutes. Getting these things ready first means your rooms go on sale straight away, with the right prices.

## What you need

| You need | Why |
| --- | --- |
| A **SiteMinder** account for this hotel | SiteMinder is the channel manager you can connect today. |
| The role **Owner**, **General manager** or **Manager** for this hotel | Only these roles can connect and disconnect. Everyone else can see the Channel manager page but can't change it. See [Users and permissions](/business/account-and-access/users-and-permissions). |
| A listing with **rooms and rate plans** in OpenBookings | SiteMinder links to the rooms and rate plans you have here. It can't create them. If the hotel has no listing yet, the Channel manager page says *No listing yet*. |
| Someone who can change settings in **SiteMinder** | You finish the connection there: add the channel, link rooms and rate plans, check two settings. |

<Callout type="info">
  OpenBookings is going through SiteMinder's certification. Until that is finished, you may not find OpenBookings in SiteMinder's list of channels. Contact support if you want to connect before then.
</Callout>

## Get your rooms and rate plans ready

SiteMinder links each of its rooms and rate plans to one of ours. Before you connect:

1. Open **About → Rooms** and check that every room type you want to sell is there and active.
2. Check that each room has the rate plans you want to sell, for example *Flexible* and *Non-refundable*, and that they're active.
3. Use names you'll recognise in SiteMinder. Linking is easiest when the names match on both sides.
4. Set **Guests included** for each room. SiteMinder's price is for that many guests. Guests above it pay the extra-adult or extra-child fee from SiteMinder.
5. Set cots and extra beds here as usual. They stay in OpenBookings and are paid at the hotel.

Inactive rooms and rate plans aren't offered to SiteMinder.

## Decide about bookings you already have

If the hotel has upcoming bookings, meaning bookings that haven't checked out yet, the connect screen asks whether to send them to SiteMinder.

- **Send them** (the default) if they aren't in SiteMinder or your PMS yet.
- **Don't send them** if you've already entered them in SiteMinder or your PMS by hand. You then keep their later changes and cancellations up to date by hand too.

See [Bookings made before you connect](/business/channel-managers/bookings-and-delivery#bookings-made-before-you-connect).

## Check how you price in SiteMinder

OpenBookings uses SiteMinder's **Per Day Pricing**: one price per rate plan per night, plus an optional extra-adult and extra-child amount. Before connecting, check that:

- Your prices in SiteMinder **include VAT** and are in **euros**.
- Your prices **don't include tourist tax**. OpenBookings adds it on top.
- Your SiteMinder **child ages** match ours: adults 13 and over, children 2 to 12.

The details are in [Settings in SiteMinder](/business/channel-managers/settings-in-siteminder).

## Limitations and warnings

- Occupancy-based pricing, Included Occupancy and Single Guest Discount from SiteMinder aren't supported.
- Only euro prices are supported.
- If you run your own installation of OpenBookings, the SiteMinder app has to be set up first. See [Environment variables](/self-hosted/reference/environment-variables#siteminder-app).
