## Overview

A channel manager keeps your prices and rooms in step across every site that sells your hotel. If you use one, you set a price once, and it goes to Booking.com, Expedia, your own website and, once connected, OpenBookings.

When you connect your channel manager to OpenBookings:

- **Your channel manager sends us** prices, rooms to sell and restrictions (stop-sells, minimum and maximum stay, closed to arrival or departure).
- **We send your channel manager** every OpenBookings booking, change and cancellation. It passes them on to your PMS and takes the room off sale on your other channels.

You keep doing the rest in OpenBookings: rooms, rate plans, photos, descriptions, policies, messages with guests and payouts.

## Which channel managers you can connect

| Channel manager | Status |
| --- | --- |
| **SiteMinder** | Supported |
| Others | Not yet |

Each channel manager connects through its own app. The app talks to OpenBookings in the same way for every channel manager, so more can be added later. If your hotel uses a different channel manager or PMS, let us know through support.

## How it works

```
Your channel manager ── prices, rooms to sell, restrictions ──▶ OpenBookings ──▶ guests
Your channel manager ◀── bookings, changes, cancellations ──── OpenBookings ◀── guests
```

- Updates from your channel manager are usually visible on OpenBookings within a minute.
- A new booking usually reaches your channel manager within a minute. Each booking shows whether it arrived.
- If a booking doesn't arrive, we email you and the booking shows what to do. Nothing is cancelled or refunded because of a connection problem.
- You still get the OpenBookings email for every new booking and cancellation, whether or not your channel manager has it.

## Where to start

1. Check [Before you connect](/business/channel-managers/before-you-connect).
2. Follow [Connecting SiteMinder](/business/channel-managers/connect-siteminder).
3. Set up SiteMinder as described in [Settings in SiteMinder](/business/channel-managers/settings-in-siteminder).
4. Read [Working while connected](/business/channel-managers/working-while-connected) so you know what changes in your dashboard.

## Limitations and warnings

- You connect one hotel at a time: the hotel you have open in the dashboard.
- A channel manager runs the whole hotel. You can't keep some rooms on OpenBookings' own rates while others come from the channel manager.
- Only one channel manager can be connected to a hotel at a time.
