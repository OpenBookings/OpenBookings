---
title: Money, tax and commission
description: How prices, tourist tax, commission and VAT relate.
---

OpenBookings operates in the EU, in euros, and every price a guest sees is the full price.

## Rates include tax

A rate entered by a hotel **includes VAT and any mandatory fees**. Every organisation confirms
that before its hotels can be booked. The one exception is **tourist tax**, which towns set
differently. It's computed from each property's tourist tax rules (a percentage of the room price,
or a fixed amount per person, night or room) and added on top. Guests see it before they pay.

## Prepayment, always

Every booking is paid in full, online, at checkout. There's no pay-at-the-hotel option. The
payment is a Stripe **direct charge** on the hotel's own connected account. The money belongs to
the hotel from the start, and OpenBookings takes its commission as an application fee.

## Commission

The commission is **4.5% of the room price**. It's never taken on tourist tax, which passes
straight to the hotel. The fee itself includes VAT: Dutch VAT for a Dutch hotel, and reverse
charge for a hotel elsewhere in the EU with a verified VAT number. Each booking stores the fee
and its net/VAT split at the moment it's made.

## Cancellation

Each property has a tier: **Flex** (free cancellation until 24 hours before arrival) or
**Standard** (free until 7 days before). A rate plan can be **non-refundable**, which overrides
the tier. When a booking is paid, the exact deadlines are worked out in the property's time zone
and stored on each reservation, so a later change to the policy never changes an existing booking.
A no-show keeps the full amount, tax included.

## Reporting

From the commission ledger, `apps/invoice-job` makes a monthly invoice per hotel, and quarterly
CSVs for the VAT return, the ICP listing (EU reverse-charge customers) and DAC7 (platform income
reporting).
