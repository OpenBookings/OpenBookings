---
title: Disconnecting and reconnecting
description: What happens to your rates, availability and bookings when you disconnect SiteMinder, and how to connect again.
---

*Last updated: 25 November 2026*

## Overview

You can disconnect at any time. Rates, availability and restrictions come back to you in OpenBookings, starting from the last values SiteMinder sent, so nothing reopens at an old price.

## How to disconnect

1. Open **About → CM**.
2. Under **Disconnect SiteMinder**, select **Disconnect**, then confirm.
3. In SiteMinder, remove OpenBookings as a channel. Otherwise it keeps sending updates that OpenBookings ignores.

Pricing rules apply again. New bookings, changes and cancellations are no longer sent to SiteMinder.

## What you get back

The values are kept for the next 750 days, or longer if SiteMinder sent anything further ahead. Night by night:

- **Availability** is set to what was left to sell when you disconnected. Nights SiteMinder never sent availability for are set to 0.
- **Prices** become price overrides labelled *Last value from SiteMinder*. Nights without a price from SiteMinder are closed, as they were while connected. Reopen them in the rates grid when you want to sell them.
- **Stop-sells, closed to arrival, closed to departure and minimum/maximum stay** become your own restrictions. A SiteMinder *through* stay limit becomes a stay limit for arrivals on that night.
- Your own overrides, restrictions and room closures on those nights are replaced. Before and after those nights they stay as they were.
- If SiteMinder never sent anything at all, nothing changes: your own values stay.
- **Extra-adult and extra-child fees** go back to the amounts in **About → Rooms**.

After disconnecting, check the [rates grid](/business/rates-and-availability/reading-the-grid) and adjust anything you want to change.

## Connecting again

Follow [Connecting SiteMinder](/business/channel-managers/connect-siteminder) again. The hotel code stays the same.

- You get the same choice for bookings made or changed while disconnected: send them, or add them by hand.
- A booking SiteMinder already has is never sent twice as a new booking. If it changed in between, the change is sent.
- Every room shows *Not linked in SiteMinder yet* until SiteMinder sends it again. Values from the earlier connection are not reused.

<Callout type="info">
  Run a **full refresh** of OpenBookings in SiteMinder right after reconnecting, so all rooms are back on sale within minutes.
</Callout>

## Limitations and warnings

- Bookings made while disconnected only reach SiteMinder if you tick **Send** when you connect again.
