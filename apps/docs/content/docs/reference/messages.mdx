## Overview

**Messages** is your inbox for guest conversations. Every guest who contacts you about your property appears as a conversation in a list on the left; selecting one opens the full exchange, where you can read the history and reply. Messages arrive live, so a new one appears without you refreshing the page.

Unlike the Overview and Reservations screens, this one works with your real conversations.

## What you can do here

- See every guest conversation, newest activity first
- See at a glance which conversations have unread guest messages, and how many
- Read the full history of a conversation
- Reply to a guest
- Share a link that opens straight to one conversation

## The conversation list

Each row shows the guest's name and photo, your property's name, when the last message was sent, and a preview of that message. When the last message was yours, the preview starts with **You:**.

A conversation with unread guest messages is shown in bold with a badge counting them. If you have no conversations yet, the list says *No conversations yet*.

## How to reply to a guest

1. Select the conversation in the list.
2. Read the exchange. Your messages appear on the right, the guest's on the left, grouped under day headings such as **Today** and **Yesterday**.
3. Type in the **Write a message...** box at the bottom.
4. Press **Enter** to send, or select the send button.

To start a new line instead of sending, press **Shift+Enter**.

## Staying connected

If the live connection drops, a **Reconnecting** label appears next to the guest's name at the top of the conversation. Messages you send are held until the connection returns — there is nothing you need to do.

## Contact details and flagged messages

Sharing phone numbers, email addresses or messaging-app handles moves the conversation off OpenBookings, which is not allowed. When a message looks like it contains contact details, it is still sent, but:

- The sender sees a warning under the conversation
- The message carries a **Flagged** label

<Callout type="warn">
  Flagging is a warning, not a block. The message is delivered either way, and the flag stays on it permanently. Keep guest conversations on the platform.
</Callout>

## How to share a link to one conversation

Open the conversation, then copy the address from your browser. Sending that link to a colleague opens the same conversation for them.

## Tips

- Work down from the top of the list — conversations are ordered by most recent activity, so anything needing attention rises to the top.
- On a phone or narrow window the conversation opens as a panel over the list; close it to get back.

## Limitations and warnings

- You can only reply within existing conversations. There is no way to start a new conversation with a guest from the dashboard.
- There is no search, filter or archive for conversations, and no way to mark one as unread again once you have opened it.
- Messages are text only — you cannot attach files or photos.
- A message can be up to 5,000 characters.

### How long messages are kept

Conversations that have had no activity for **12 months** are cleared automatically: the text is replaced with a note saying the retention period expired, and the sender is no longer recorded.

<Callout type="warn">
  Clearing old conversations cannot be undone. If you need a record of an exchange for longer than a year, save it elsewhere before the year is up.
</Callout>
