## Overview

**Overview** is the landing screen of the dashboard and the one to check at the start of a shift. It answers three questions immediately: who is leaving today, who is arriving today, and how many guests are currently on the property.

## What you can do here

- See counts for today's check-outs, today's check-ins, guests in house, and upcoming reservations
- Switch between those four lists and read the reservations in each
- Open any reservation to see its details

## The four cards

Across the top are four counters:

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **C/O's Today** | Reservations checking out today. |
| **C/I's Today** | Reservations checking in today. |
| **In-house** | Guests currently staying — this counts people, not reservations. |
| **Upcoming** | Reservations arriving on a future date. |

Note the difference on **In-house**: a reservation for a family of four adds four to that number, while the other three cards count reservations.

## The reservation lists

Below the cards are four tabs — **Upcoming**, **Check-ins**, **Check-outs** and **In-house** — each showing a count beside its name. Select a tab to see the matching reservations in a table with the reservation number, guest, room, dates, number of guests, status and total.

When a list is empty it says so plainly, for example *No check-ins today*.

## How to look up a guest's reservation

1. Select the tab that matches their stage — **Check-ins** for someone arriving today, **In-house** for someone already staying.
2. Find their name in the table.
3. For the full record, go to **Bookings → Reservations** and select the row there.

## Tips

- The counts are calculated from today's date, so the screen is correct without you refreshing it at the start of a shift.
- To act on a reservation rather than just read it, work from [Reservations](/docs/reference/reservations) instead.

## Limitations and warnings

<Callout type="warn">
  The reservations shown on this screen are sample data, not your property's real bookings. Treat the Overview as a preview of the layout until live reservations are connected.
</Callout>

- Rows on this screen cannot be selected to open a detail panel. Use the Reservations page for that.
