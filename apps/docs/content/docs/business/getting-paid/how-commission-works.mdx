## Overview

OpenBookings charges a commission on each booking. It covers the guest discovery platform and business tooling.

## The calculation

Commission is **4.5% of the room price**, including VAT: the rates you set, after any adjustments and discounts, plus any extra-guest charges. Your rates include VAT, so there is no separate pre-VAT amount.

Commission is never charged on:

- **Tourist tax.** It is added on top of the room price for you to pay to your municipality.
- **Cots and extra beds.** The guest pays for these at your property, not through OpenBookings.

For example, a stay with a room price of €300 and €12 tourist tax costs the guest €312. The commission is 4.5% of €300: €13.50.

The commission is worked out per booking, rounded to the nearest cent, and fixed when the guest books.

It is taken automatically when the guest pays. You receive a monthly invoice for your records; there is nothing to pay.

The invoice is a VAT invoice for the commission already collected. If your business is in the Netherlands, the commission includes 21% Dutch VAT. If it is in another EU country, VAT is reverse charged and you account for it yourself. See [Your statement](/business/getting-paid/your-statement) for what is on it and where to download it.

## Payment processing fees

Guest payments are made directly to your own Stripe account. Stripe charges its processing fee on each payment and deducts it from your Stripe balance. That fee is Stripe's, not OpenBookings', and is separate from the commission.

## Refunds

If a booking is refunded, the commission is returned to your Stripe account in proportion to the room price the guest gets back: if the guest gets 80% of the room price back, you get 80% of the commission back, and a full refund of the room price returns all of it. Tourist tax never counts. Refunding tourist tax returns no commission, because none was charged on it. Stripe does not return its own processing fee on a refund.

### Refund through OpenBookings

Always refund a guest through OpenBookings: cancel or record an early departure on the reservation, or ask support for a goodwill refund. Then every refund says how much was room price and how much was tourist tax, and the commission follows the room price exactly.

A refund you make directly in your Stripe Dashboard happens outside OpenBookings. We can't see what it was for, so:

- no commission comes back on it. Stripe works the same way: it keeps its own processing fee on a refund;
- you are responsible for the tourist tax on it. Tourist tax is yours to pay to the municipality, and only you know whether that refund changes what you owe;
- we may ask you to explain the refund.

A lost dispute is different, because you didn't choose it: the bank takes money back without saying what it was for, so we return commission in proportion to the whole payment, tourist tax included. You stay responsible for the tourist tax on the disputed amount.

## Changes to the rate

Any change to the commission rate requires 30 days' notice before taking effect, and never applies to bookings already made.
