*Last updated: 25 November 2026*

## Overview

If you use SiteMinder as your channel manager, you can connect it to OpenBookings. SiteMinder then sends your prices, rooms to sell and restrictions to OpenBookings, and every OpenBookings booking, change and cancellation goes to SiteMinder, which passes it on to your PMS.

You connect one hotel at a time: the hotel you have open in the dashboard. Owners, general managers and managers of this hotel can connect and disconnect. Everyone else sees the page but can't change it.

## How to connect

1. Open **About → CM** in the sidebar. This is the Channel manager page.
2. Read **What changes once connected**.
3. If the hotel has upcoming bookings, decide on **Send your N upcoming bookings to SiteMinder**. See [Bookings made before you connect](#bookings-made-before-you-connect).
4. Select **Connect SiteMinder**.
5. The page shows your **Hotel code**. Select the copy button next to it.
6. In SiteMinder, add the OpenBookings channel and enter this hotel code.
7. In SiteMinder, link each room and rate plan to the one with the same name in OpenBookings.
8. Back on the Channel manager page, check **Rooms linked**. Each room goes on sale on OpenBookings as soon as SiteMinder has sent it a price and availability.

The page says **Waiting for SiteMinder** until the first update arrives, then **Connected**.

## Settings to check in SiteMinder

Two settings in SiteMinder need to match how OpenBookings works.

**Child ages.** On OpenBookings, adults are 13 and over and children are 2 to 12. Infants under 2 are not counted as guests. Set the same ages in SiteMinder, or the guests SiteMinder prices for won't be the guests who book.

**Auto-Replenishment.** Turn it on. Without it, SiteMinder doesn't free the room when a booking is cancelled, so a cancelled OpenBookings booking keeps the room off sale on every channel until you put it back by hand.

<Callout type="warn">
  Enter prices in SiteMinder **including VAT**, in euros, as you would here. Don't include tourist tax: OpenBookings adds it on top. See [How pricing works](/business/rates-and-availability/how-pricing-works).
</Callout>

## What changes once connected

- **Rates & availability is read-only.** The grid still shows prices, rooms to sell and restrictions, now as SiteMinder sent them. You change them in SiteMinder.
- **Pricing rules don't apply** to rates SiteMinder manages. The Pricing rules page marks them *Not applied to rates managed by SiteMinder*. See [Running promotions](#running-promotions).
- **Extra-adult and extra-child fees come from SiteMinder**, with each night's price. In **About → Rooms** those fields show *Set in SiteMinder*. Guests included, cots and extra beds stay here.
- **A room is not on sale until SiteMinder has sent it availability and a price.** Until then the grid marks it *Not linked in SiteMinder yet · not on sale*. Link it in SiteMinder to put it on sale.
- **Every booking goes to SiteMinder**, and each booking shows whether it arrived. See [Delivery status](#delivery-status).
- **Rooms, rate plans, photos and descriptions stay here.** You still add and edit them in **About → Rooms**.

You still get the OpenBookings email for every new booking and cancellation, whether or not SiteMinder has it.

## Bookings made before you connect

When the hotel has upcoming bookings (bookings that haven't checked out yet), the connect screen shows a tick box: **Send your N upcoming bookings to SiteMinder**. It is ticked by default.

- **Ticked:** those bookings go to SiteMinder as new bookings, and their changes and cancellations follow.
- **Unticked:** use this if you've already added them to SiteMinder or your PMS by hand. They are marked **Not sent** and stay that way: their later changes and cancellations aren't sent either, so keep them up to date by hand.

If you disconnect and connect again, you get the same choice for the bookings made or changed in between. A booking SiteMinder already has is never sent twice as a new booking.

After connecting again, every room shows *Not linked in SiteMinder yet* until SiteMinder sends it again: values from the earlier connection are not reused. Run a **full refresh** of OpenBookings in SiteMinder right after reconnecting, so all rooms are back on sale within minutes.

## Delivery status

Open a booking in **Bookings → Reservations**. At the end of the panel, the **SiteMinder** section shows where it stands:

| Status | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sent** and a time | SiteMinder confirmed it at that time. | Nothing. |
| **Sending** | On its way. This usually takes under a minute. Where instant notifications (QStash) aren't set up, it can take up to the polling interval: 5 minutes by default. | Nothing. |
| **Retrying** | SiteMinder didn't answer. We keep trying for up to 30 minutes. | Nothing yet. |
| **Not delivered** | SiteMinder didn't accept it, or kept not answering. | Add it by hand (see below). |
| **Not sent** | Made before you connected, and you chose to add these bookings by hand. | Nothing. |

A change or cancellation is sent the same way, and the status shows how the latest one went.

When a booking is **Not delivered**, the top of the panel says: *Not in SiteMinder yet. Add it in SiteMinder or your PMS by hand, or check the connection.* The booking itself is confirmed and paid: nothing is cancelled or refunded.

## The email when a booking doesn't arrive

When a booking can't be delivered, we also email the hotel's team, with the subject *Booking OB-… didn't reach SiteMinder*. It names the booking and the dates, and what SiteMinder said if it gave a reason. Add the booking to SiteMinder or your PMS by hand, or check the connection on the Channel manager page. The Channel manager page also counts the bookings that need attention.

## Running promotions

While SiteMinder is connected, discounts and special prices are set in SiteMinder. Our pricing rules (length of stay, early booking, last minute and day-of-week adjustments) don't apply to rates SiteMinder manages, so the guest pays the price SiteMinder sent.

To offer a deal only on OpenBookings:

1. In **About → Rooms**, add a separate rate plan to the room, for example *Book direct*.
2. In SiteMinder, link it to a rate plan of its own.
3. Set its price in SiteMinder. It goes on sale once SiteMinder has sent a price for it.

## How to disconnect

1. Open **About → CM**.
2. Under **Disconnect SiteMinder**, select **Disconnect**, then confirm.
3. In SiteMinder, remove OpenBookings as a channel. Otherwise it keeps sending updates that OpenBookings ignores.

Rates, availability and restrictions come back to you on the grid, starting from the last values SiteMinder sent. Pricing rules apply again. New bookings, changes and cancellations are no longer sent.

What that means night by night, from today up to the last night SiteMinder sent anything for:

- **Availability** is set to what was left to sell when you disconnected. Nights SiteMinder never sent availability for are set to 0.
- **Prices** become price overrides labelled *Last value from SiteMinder*. Nights without a price from SiteMinder are closed.
- **Stop-sells, closed to arrival, closed to departure and minimum/maximum stay** become your own restrictions. A SiteMinder *through* stay limit becomes a stay limit for arrivals on that night.
- Your own overrides, restrictions and room closures on those nights are replaced. Before and after those nights they stay as they were.
- **Extra-adult and extra-child fees** go back to the amounts in **About → Rooms**.

## Limitations and warnings

<Callout type="warn">
  A booking that is **Not delivered** is not in SiteMinder, so your other channels may still sell that room. Add it by hand as soon as you see it.
</Callout>

- SiteMinder runs the whole hotel. You can't keep some rooms on OpenBookings' own rates while others come from SiteMinder.
- Only SiteMinder can be connected for now.
- The hotel code never changes. Keep it as it is in SiteMinder.
- Guest contact details (email, phone, address) are not sent to SiteMinder. Bookings arrive with first and last names only.
