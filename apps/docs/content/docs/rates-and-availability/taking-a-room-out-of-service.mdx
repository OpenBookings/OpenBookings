title: Taking a room out of service
description: How to block rooms for maintenance without breaking existing rates.

*Last updated: 10 September 2026*

## Overview

Use **blocked units**, not an availability override, to hold a room back for maintenance or owner use. Blocking removes rooms from the pool while letting the calculation keep running, so the number stays correct as new bookings arrive.

## How to take a room out of service

1. Open Rates & Availability and select **Edit availability** on the toolbar.
2. Choose the **Room type**.
3. Set **From** and **To** to cover the full period.
4. In **Blocked units**, enter how many rooms are out of service. For one room, enter `1`. If rooms are already blocked for another reason, enter the total.
5. Leave **Availability override** and **Capacity override** empty.
6. In **Note**, write what is happening and when it ends.
7. Select **Apply to range**.

## How to put the room back

Repeat the steps for the same room type and dates, setting **Blocked units** back to `0` — or to whatever is still legitimately blocked. Update the note.

## Limitations and warnings

- Blocked units do not expire on their own. Nothing releases the rooms until you do. 
- Blocking affects every rate plan on that room type.
- The dashboard tracks room types, not individual rooms. Blocking one unit removes *a* room from the pool; it does not reserve a specific room number.
- If the room type already has bookings that would now exceed what is left, blocking does not cancel them.
