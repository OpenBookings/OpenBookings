# Trademark Policy

The OpenBookings source code is open source. The OpenBookings **name, logo and visual identity**
(the *marks*) are not. They belong to Wouter van der Wal, and this policy explains how you may
and may not use them.

The goal is simple: anyone who sees the OpenBookings name should be able to trust that it means
the official project or the official hosted service, and not someone else's fork.

## You may, without asking

- Use the code under the terms of the [LICENSE](./LICENSE), including in a fork.
- Say truthfully that your software is *based on* or *a fork of* OpenBookings, or *compatible with*
  OpenBookings, in plain text and without the logo.
- Use the name to refer to the project in articles, talks, reviews and tutorials.
- Self-host an unmodified copy for your own hotel or your own organisation, and call it OpenBookings
  internally.
- Fork the repository to send pull requests.

## You may not, without written permission

- Use **OpenBookings**, the logo, or anything confusingly similar in the name of a product,
  service, company, domain name or app.
- Offer a hosted or managed service under the OpenBookings name, or with its logo.
- Suggest that a fork, product or service is official, endorsed, certified or supported by the
  project.
- Change the logo, or combine it with other marks.

## Forks and hosted services

If you distribute a modified version or run it as a service for others, you must:

- give it a different name, logo and visual identity,
- remove the OpenBookings logo and branding from the user interface, emails, invoices and
  documentation, and
- state clearly that it is not affiliated with or endorsed by the OpenBookings project.

Keeping the license and copyright notices is still required. That's a separate obligation under
the [LICENSE](./LICENSE), not a use of the marks.

## Asking for permission

To ask for permission, or to report misuse of the marks, email support@openbookings.co with
`Trademark` in the subject line.

This policy may be updated. The version in the `main` branch of the repository applies.
