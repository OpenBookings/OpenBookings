## Overview

The dashboard shares a consistent frame: a sidebar down the left for moving between sections, and a top bar showing where you are with a search box on the right.

## The sidebar

The sidebar carries your property name at the top, and groups the main sections:

- **Overview** — Today's arrivals, departures and in-house guests.
- **Bookings** — Groups **Reservations**, **Messages** and **Reviews**.
- **Property** — Groups **R&A** (rates and availability), **Property** and **Rooms**.
- **Finance** — Payouts and money matters.
- **Analytics** — Performance reporting.

Select the control next to your property name to collapse the sidebar into a narrow strip of icons, giving screens like the rates grid more room.

## The top bar

The left of the top bar shows a breadcrumb trail of where you are, such as *Bookings → Reservations*. Earlier parts of the trail are links, letting you step back up a level.

## Search and the command menu

On the right of the top bar is a **Search...** box. Select it, or press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows), to open the command menu. 

You use the command menu to jump straight to any section by typing its name, or to find a reservation by typing two or more characters of a guest name or reservation number. 

<Callout type="warn">
  Several command menu entries are placeholders today. **New Reservation**, **Check In Guest**, **Check Out Guest**, **Account Settings**, **Switch Property**, **Change Theme**, **Search Help Center**, **Send Feedback** and **Contact Support** currently close the menu without doing anything.
</Callout>
