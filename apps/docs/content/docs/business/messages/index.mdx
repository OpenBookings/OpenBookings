## Overview

**Messages** is your inbox for guest conversations. Every guest who contacts you appears as a conversation in a list on the left. Messages arrive live without you refreshing the page.

## How to reply to a guest

1. Select the conversation in the list.
2. Read the exchange. Your messages appear on the right, the guest's on the left.
3. Type in the **Write a message...** box at the bottom.
4. Press **Enter** to send, or select the send button. (Press **Shift+Enter** for a new line.)

## How to share a link to one conversation

Open the conversation, then copy the address from your browser. Sending that link to a colleague opens the exact same conversation for them.

## Contact details

When a message appears to contain a phone number, email address, or messaging handle, it is still delivered but receives a permanent **Flagged** label.

<Callout type="warn">
  The sender sees this warning: *"This message may contain contact info. Sharing contact details off-platform isn't allowed."* Keep guest conversations on the platform.
</Callout>

## Limitations and warnings

- You can only reply within existing conversations. You cannot start a new conversation with a guest from the dashboard.
- There is no search, filter or archive for conversations.
- You cannot mark a conversation as unread once opened.
- Messages are text only — you cannot attach files or photos.
- A message body cannot exceed 5,000 characters.
- Conversations with no activity for **365 days** are cleared automatically. The text is replaced with a retention expiry note, and the sender is anonymised. This cannot be undone.
