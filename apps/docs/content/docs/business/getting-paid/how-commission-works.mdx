## Overview

OpenBookings charges a commission on each booking. It covers the guest discovery platform and business tooling.

## The calculation

Commission is **4.5% of the booking total**: the full price the guest pays. Your rates include tax, so there is no separate pre-tax amount, and the commission is calculated on the whole price.

It is taken automatically when the guest pays. You do not receive a separate bill to settle.

## Payment processing fees

Guest payments are made directly to your own Stripe account. Stripe charges its processing fee on each payment and deducts it from your Stripe balance. That fee is Stripe's, not OpenBookings', and is separate from the commission.

## Refunds

If a booking is refunded, the commission is returned to your Stripe account in the same proportion: a full refund returns all of it, a half refund returns half. Stripe does not return its own processing fee on a refund.

## Changes to the rate

Any change to the commission rate requires 30 days' notice before taking effect, and never applies to bookings already made.
