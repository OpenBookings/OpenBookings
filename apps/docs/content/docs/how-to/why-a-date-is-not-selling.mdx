title: Why a date is not selling
description: Work out which of the three causes is behind a date guests cannot book.
---

## Overview

A guest says they cannot book a date, or you notice a date sitting empty. There are only three possible causes, and the grid tells you which one you are looking at. Work through them in order — the fix is different each time, and applying the wrong one wastes an afternoon.

## Before you start

Open [Rates & Availability](/docs/reference/rates-and-availability), set the date range so the date in question is visible, and expand the room type involved.

## Step 1 — Check the rooms first

Look at the **Available rooms** row for that room type on that date.

**If it shows 0 (in red)**, you are sold out. Every rate plan on that room type is unsellable, and no amount of reopening will change it.

- If you are genuinely full, nothing is wrong.
- If you should have rooms, select the number and read **How this number is reached**. Check whether **Blocked** is higher than expected, or whether a manual override is holding the figure down. Fix it with [Editing availability](/docs/how-to/editing-availability).

**If it shows a number greater than zero**, rooms exist. Move to step 2.

## Step 2 — Check the rate plan for a closure

Look at the rate plan row for that date.

**If you see a red Closed bar**, someone closed this plan. Select the bar — the panel names who closed it, when, and any note they left. If it should be open, select **Reopen these dates**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Reopening lifts the closure only. If a stay rule was set over the same dates, it survives — carry on to step 3 rather than assuming the date is fixed.
</Callout>

**If there is no red bar**, move to step 3.

## Step 3 — Check for stay rules

Look for an amber **Restricted** bar.

Select it, and the panel spells out in plain language what the rule does — for example *guests must book at least 3 nights to include these dates*. Compare that against the stay the guest is trying to book:

| The rule | Blocks a guest who... |
| --- | --- |
| **Minimum stay** | wants a shorter stay than the minimum. |
| **Maximum stay** | wants a longer stay than the maximum. |
| **Closed to arrival** | wants to *check in* on that date. A guest arriving earlier and staying through is fine. |
| **Closed to departure** | wants to *check out* on that date. |

To change a rule, set a new one over the same dates from [Setting restrictions](/docs/how-to/setting-restrictions).

## Step 4 — Check the other rate plans

If the plan you were looking at is genuinely open, check the other plans on the same room type. A guest may have been looking at a plan whose conditions they do not meet while a different plan would sell to them perfectly well.

The coloured dot beside each plan name summarises the dates in view: green means all open, amber means some dates closed or restricted, red means nothing bookable.

## Quick summary

| What you see | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Red 0 in **Available rooms** | Sold out or over-blocked | Editing availability |
| Red bar on the plan row | Closed deliberately | Reopen these dates |
| Amber bar on the plan row | Stay rule not met | Set a new restriction |
| Everything looks open | Guest was on a different plan, or booking a stay length that does not qualify | Check the other plans |

## Tips

- Turn on **Show only with issues** in the Filter menu to see every room with something closed, restricted or sold out in one pass.
- If a price looks wrong rather than missing, that is a different question — see [Managing rate plans](/docs/how-to/managing-rate-plans).
