## Overview

**Rates & Availability** — shown as **R&A** in the sidebar — is the calendar grid where you control what you sell. Dates run across the top, room types run down the side, and every square tells you either how many rooms are free or what a rate plan costs on that date.

This page is about reading and moving around the grid. The editing tools are covered in [Editing availability](/docs/property/editing-availability), [Managing rate plans](/docs/property/managing-rate-plans) and [Setting restrictions](/docs/property/setting-restrictions).

<Callout type="warn">
  This screen is for a large screen only. On a phone or narrow window it shows *Open this on a desktop* instead of the grid. This is deliberate — a wrong tap on a cramped grid can close rooms you did not mean to close.
</Callout>

## What you can do here

- Move through dates a week, fortnight or month at a time
- Switch between a 7, 14 or 30 day view
- See what a rate would be for a longer stay
- Expand a room type to see its individual rate plans
- Hide room types you are not working on, or show only ones needing attention
- Select any square to see exactly how its number was arrived at

## How the grid is laid out

The layout mirrors [how your property is organised](/docs/concepts/how-your-property-is-organised): the shared pool of rooms on top, the ways of selling it underneath.

**Collapsed**, a room type is a single row showing the room name, a summary such as *2/4 plans open*, and the number of rooms available on each date.

**Expanded**, the room name moves to a narrow band down the left — where it stays visible as you scroll sideways — and the rows become:

- **Available rooms**, showing the number free on each date, with the total unit count on the right
- One row per rate plan, showing the price on each date

Select the arrow at the start of a room's row to expand or collapse it. Room types start collapsed, because six room types with four plans each would otherwise open as two dozen rows.

Today's column is tinted and labelled in the highlight colour. Weekends are tinted more faintly, so you can see the shape of a week at a glance.

## Reading the cells

The full legend is in [Reference → Grid legend](/docs/reference/grid-legend). In short:

| Cell | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| A number | Rooms free — amber at two or fewer, red at none, with a dot if manually overridden. |
| A price | The plan is open. An asterisk means a longer-stay discount is not included. |
| A red bar | **Closed** — you closed this plan. |
| A grey striped bar | **Sold out** — the room type is full. |
| An amber bar | **Restricted** — bookable with conditions, named on the bar. |

Telling **Closed** from **Sold out** matters, and [Restrictions and closures](/docs/concepts/restrictions-and-closures) explains why.

## How to move through dates

1. Use the arrows either side of **Today** to step back or forward by exactly the period you are viewing.
2. Select **Today** to jump back to the current date.
3. Use the date range list to switch between **Next 7 days**, **Next 14 days** and **Next 30 days**.

The dates you are viewing are part of the page address, so you can bookmark a week or send it to a colleague, and your browser's back button steps back through the weeks you looked at.

## How to see rates for a longer stay

Use the second list on the toolbar — **Rates for 1 night**, **3 nights** or **7 nights** — to see what a guest booking that long would be charged. The default is one night. See [Pricing](/docs/concepts/pricing) for why this changes the number.

## How to focus on the rooms you care about

1. Select **Filter**.
2. Clear the tick beside any room type you want out of the way.
3. Optionally tick **Show only with issues** to see only rooms with something closed, restricted or sold out.
4. The **Filter** button shows how many filters are active.

To clear them, select the filter icon that appears next to the button.

Filters stay on as you move between weeks, so "hide the rooms I am not working on" holds for a whole planning session.

## Moving around with the keyboard

The grid is one stop when tabbing through the page. Once inside it, arrow keys move a cell at a time, **Home** and **End** jump to the first or last date, and **Page Up** and **Page Down** jump to the first or last row. See [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/reference/grid-legend#keyboard-shortcuts).

## Tips

- Hover a price to see how it was worked out without opening the panel. Use it to audit a week quickly; open the panel when you are about to make a decision.
- **Show only with issues** is the fastest way to start a planning session — it surfaces exactly the dates needing a decision.
- If the screen says *No room types yet*, your property has no room types or rate plans set up.

## Limitations and warnings

<Callout type="warn">
  Every change made here affects what guests can book, immediately.
</Callout>

- You can only view 7, 14 or 30 day periods, and only 1, 3 or 7 night stay lengths. Step forward with the arrows to plan further out.
- Selecting a cell shows information; it does not let you edit in place. All changes go through the toolbar buttons.
