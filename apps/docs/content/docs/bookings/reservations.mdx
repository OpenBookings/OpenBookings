## Overview

**Reservations** is the full list of bookings for your property. Where the Overview screen gives you a summary, this is where you come to look a booking up, check the dates and room, and read what the guest is paying.

## What you can do here

- Switch between four lists: Upcoming, Check-ins, Check-outs and In-house
- Read every reservation's number, guest, room, dates, party size, status and total
- Open a reservation to see the guest, the stay and a night-by-night price breakdown
- Share a link that opens straight to one reservation

## The four lists

At the top left is a button showing the list you are viewing and how many reservations are in it. Select it to switch:

| List | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Upcoming** | Reservations arriving on a future date. |
| **Check-ins** | Reservations arriving today. |
| **Check-outs** | Reservations leaving today. |
| **In-house** | Guests currently staying. |

Each option shows its own count, so you can see how busy a list is before opening it.

## Reservation statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | The booking needs a decision or is not yet confirmed. |
| **Confirmed** | Booked and expected. |
| **Checked in** | The guest is on the property. |
| **Checked out** | The stay is finished. |

## How to open a reservation

1. Select any row in the table.
2. A details panel opens — from the right on a large screen, from the bottom on a smaller one.
3. Select the same row again, or the cross in the panel's corner, to close it.

The panel shows:

- **Guest** — name and party size
- **Stay** — room, check-in date, check-out date and number of nights
- **Payment** — what the guest pays for each night, and the total

## How to share a link to one reservation

Open the reservation, then copy the address from your browser. The address includes the reservation, so sending it to a colleague opens the same panel for them. The same link works if you bookmark it.

## Tips

- Press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows) and type a reservation number or guest name to jump straight to a booking from anywhere in the dashboard.
- The nightly rows in the Payment section always add up to the total shown beneath them, which makes them safe to read out to a guest querying their bill.

## Limitations and warnings

<Callout type="warn">
  The four buttons at the bottom of the details panel — **Edit details**, **Message**, **Mark no-show** and **Request Cancellation** — are not yet connected. Selecting them does nothing. Creating, changing, cancelling and checking guests in or out are not available in the dashboard yet.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  The reservations listed are sample data, not your property's real bookings.
</Callout>

- There is no free-text search or filter on this screen beyond the four lists. Use the command menu to find a specific booking.
- Amounts are shown in euros.
