*Last updated: 16 November 2026*

## Overview

A price is never just a number you typed. It starts from the rate plan's base rate and is moved by adjustments that fire only when their conditions are met. 

## Rates include VAT, not tourist tax

Enter every rate **including VAT and any mandatory fees**. Don't include tourist tax: OpenBookings adds it on top of the room price, worked out from the tourist tax rules you set up for your property, and shows it to the guest as a separate line. The guest pays your price plus that tourist tax.

Collecting and paying VAT and tourist tax to the authorities is your responsibility as the host.

Before your organisation can set or change a price, an owner or admin has to confirm this once. Until then prices are locked and guests cannot book; closing dates and changing restrictions still work.

## The base rate

Every rate plan has a **base rate** — the starting point for every date. A rate override replaces the base rate on particular dates. 

## The adjustments

On top of the base rate sit adjustments (such as a weekend uplift or a longer-stay discount). **Days of the week** and **Last minute** adjustments are applied to each night first. Then at most one discount (**Length of stay** or **Early booking**) is taken off: the one that saves the guest the most. The order in which you added the rules does not change the result.

## Checking prices for longer stays

The grid shows the price for a one-night stay by default, and marks any plan with a longer-stay discount waiting with an asterisk (`*`). 

To see what a guest booking longer would be charged, change the stay length list on the toolbar to **Rates for 3 nights** or **Rates for 7 nights**.

## Limitations and warnings

- Prices are in euros. A booking total below €0.50 (the smallest amount Stripe can charge) is rejected at checkout.
