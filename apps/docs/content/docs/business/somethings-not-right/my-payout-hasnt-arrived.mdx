## Overview

Payouts are sent by Stripe from your own Stripe account to your bank. OpenBookings does not hold or release your money, so the place to check is your Stripe Dashboard.

## How to check

1. Sign in at [dashboard.stripe.com](https://dashboard.stripe.com) and open **Payouts**. Each payout shows its status and expected arrival date.
2. Check your payout schedule there. A payment only becomes part of a payout once Stripe has made it available, which can take a few days, and longer for a new account.
3. Check whether Stripe is asking for anything. If Stripe needs more information about you or your business, it pauses payouts until you provide it, and says so at the top of the dashboard.
4. Check the bank account on file. A payout sent to a closed or mistyped account fails and is returned to your Stripe balance.

If the payout shows as paid in Stripe but has not reached your bank after the expected date, contact Stripe support or your bank with the payout's reference.
