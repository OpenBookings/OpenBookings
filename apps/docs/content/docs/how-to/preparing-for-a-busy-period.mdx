---
title: Preparing for a busy period
description: A checklist to run before a weekend or season you expect to fill.
---

## Overview

Before a period you expect to sell out — a festival weekend, a school holiday, high season — it is worth twenty minutes checking that nothing is quietly closed and that your stay rules say what you want them to say. Dates closed last season and never reopened are the classic way to lose a full weekend.

## The checklist

### 1. Bring the whole period into view

Open [Rates & Availability](/docs/reference/rates-and-availability) and use the date range list to show **Next 30 days**, then step forward with the arrows until the period is on screen.

### 2. Surface everything needing attention

Select **Filter** and tick **Show only with issues**. The grid now shows only room types with something closed, restricted or sold out. If nothing appears, your inventory is clear and you can stop here.

### 3. Work through what is left

For each room type still showing, expand it and look at the bars:

| Bar | Ask yourself |
| --- | --- |
| Red **Closed** | Did I mean to close this, or is it left over? Select it — the panel says who closed it and when. |
| Amber **Restricted** | Is this stay rule still what I want for a busy period? |
| Grey striped **Sold out** | Genuinely full, or over-blocked? Check the blocked figure. |

Reopen or adjust anything that no longer applies. See [Setting restrictions](/docs/how-to/setting-restrictions).

### 4. Check your minimum stays

A busy period is when minimum stays earn their keep — they stop a two-night weekend being broken up by a one-night booking. Select a rate plan cell and read **Minimum stay** under **Rate plan**.

If you want to raise it for the period, set a new restriction across those dates.

### 5. Check what a longer stay actually costs

Switch the stay length list to **Rates for 3 nights**. If prices drop noticeably, a longer-stay discount is firing — which may not be what you want when demand is high. The price breakdown shows which adjustment is responsible. See [Pricing](/docs/explanation/pricing).

### 6. Clear your filters

Select the filter icon next to the **Filter** button. Filters persist as you move between weeks, so leaving **Show only with issues** on will hide healthy rooms next time you open the screen and make it look like half your property has vanished.

## Tips

- Do this a few weeks ahead. Reopening dates the night before a festival is too late for guests who already looked and moved on.
- Leave notes when you close dates deliberately, so this check is quick next time.

## Limitations and warnings

<Callout type="warn">
  Longer-stay discounts and other price adjustments cannot be turned off from the dashboard. If a discount is firing when you would rather it did not, the only lever available is the restrictions on the plan.
</Callout>
