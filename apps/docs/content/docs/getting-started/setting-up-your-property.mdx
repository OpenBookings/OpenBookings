## Overview

The first time you sign in, you are taken through a short setup instead of the dashboard. It collects what OpenBookings needs to show your property to guests, invoice correctly, and pay you. There are four steps, and your progress is saved after each one, so you can stop and come back later.

Until all four steps are finished, the dashboard is not available — if you try to open it you are returned to setup. Once setup is complete you cannot re-enter it; from then on you change these details from within the dashboard.

## What you can do here

- Describe your property and upload photos
- Set your address and pin your exact location on a map
- Provide your company's legal details and sign the Partner Agreement
- Connect the payment account that receives your payouts

## Step 1 — Tell us about your property

This is what guests will see on OpenBookings.

1. Enter your **Property name**. This is required and is the name guests see.
2. Optionally add a **Tagline** — a short line that appears under your property name.
3. Write a **Description** covering the location, the feel of the place and what you offer.
4. Add **Property photos**. Select the upload area to browse, or drag files onto it. PNG, JPG and WEBP files up to 10 MB each are accepted. Select the small cross on a photo to remove it.
5. Optionally add your **House rules**. You can type them in, drag a plain text file onto the box, or use **Import .txt** to pick one.
6. Select the button at the bottom right to continue.

## Step 2 — Where is your property located?

This is used to place your property on the map.

1. Start typing your address in **Search address**. Suggestions appear after a few characters.
2. Choose the matching suggestion. The map moves to that spot and drops a pin.
3. Check the **Street address**, **City**, **Postal code** and **Country** fields that appear, and correct anything that came through wrong.
4. Continue to the next step.

<Callout type="info">
  The address you pick sets the map pin guests see. If the suggestion lands slightly off, fix the address fields before moving on.
</Callout>

## Step 3 — Legal and agreements

These details go on the agreement between your company and OpenBookings.

1. Fill in **Legal Company Name**, **Full Name**, **Role / Title** and **VAT Number**. All four are required.
2. Add your **CoC Number** (chamber of commerce registration) if you have one. This one is optional.
3. Select **Sign the Partner Agreement**. The button stays greyed out until the required fields above are filled.
4. A confirmation window shows who you are signing as, for which company, and in what role. Read it, then select **Sign Document**.
5. The button changes to **Signed & Sent**. Selecting it again shows the confirmation that your agreement is on file.

<Callout type="warn">
  Signing is final. Once the Partner Agreement is signed, the legal detail fields are locked and cannot be edited. Check the company name, your name, your role and the VAT number carefully before you sign.
</Callout>

Your IP address is recorded at the moment of signing and stored as part of the legally binding record. The signing window tells you this before you confirm.

## Step 4 — Set up payouts

The last step connects the payment account that receives your money. Payments are handled by Stripe.

1. Select **Continue to Stripe**. You are taken to Stripe's own secure onboarding.
2. Complete the details Stripe asks for and return to OpenBookings.
3. The page checks your status and updates by itself — you do not need to refresh it.

You will see one of three things:

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A few more details are needed** | Stripe still wants information from you. | Select **Continue to Stripe** and finish the form. |
| **Your account is under review** | Everything is submitted and Stripe is checking it. This usually takes a few minutes. | Wait — the page updates on its own. |
| You are moved to the dashboard | Your account is approved and setup is complete. | Nothing. You are ready to take bookings. |

## Tips

- The note at the bottom of the setup screen — *Progress saved after each step* — is accurate. Closing the window will not lose completed steps.
- Use **Back** to review an earlier step before you have signed the agreement.

## Limitations and warnings

- Setup must be completed in order. You cannot skip ahead to payouts before the earlier steps are done.
- Once setup is complete, the setup flow is closed permanently. Later changes to your property details are made from the dashboard.
- You cannot accept bookings until Stripe has approved your account.
