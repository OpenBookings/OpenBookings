<Section id="cancellations" number="8" title="Cancellations, changes and refunds">

**8.1 Your booking's cancellation policy.** Every rate has a cancellation policy, which the property chooses from our standard set. You see it before you book and in your confirmation. That policy decides whether you get money back and how much. The policy that applied when you booked stays in force, even if the property changes it later.

**8.2 How to cancel.** Cancel in your account, or through our support chat. We record the time of your cancellation, and deadlines are counted in the property's local time.

If you agree a cancellation or change directly with the property, that agreement is between you and the property. We can only process a refund through OpenBookings once the property has recorded it with us.

**8.3 Changes.** To change your dates or room, contact us or the property. A change is only confirmed once the property agrees, and it may change the price.

**8.4 No-shows.** If you don't arrive and haven't cancelled, the no-show rule of your cancellation policy applies. For most rates this means you pay the full price. If you'll arrive late, tell the property.

**8.5 Refunds.** Refunds are paid by the property to your original payment method. They usually arrive within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. We confirm by email once a refund has been issued.

**8.6 Checking our decisions.** We apply cancellation policies automatically. If you think we applied a policy incorrectly, contact support and ask for a review. A person will look at it and reply within 5 business days.

**8.7 Exceptional circumstances.** If you can't travel because of something serious, such as illness or a travel ban, you can ask for an exception. We pass your request to the property, and the property decides. This doesn't affect any rights you have under the law.

**8.8 If the property cancels.** If the property cancels your booking, you get a full refund of everything you paid, paid by the property. Where we can, we help you find somewhere else to stay.

</Section>
