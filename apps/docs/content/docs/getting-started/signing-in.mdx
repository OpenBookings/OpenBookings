## Overview

The dashboard sits behind a sign-in screen headed **Business Portal**. You can sign in with a link sent to your email, or with a Google or Microsoft account. There is no password to remember or reset — sign-in is either a one-time email link or your existing Google or Microsoft account.

Only business accounts can get in. If you sign in with an account that is not set up as a business account, you are returned to the sign-in screen with an error and signed out again.

## What you can do here

- Sign in with a link sent to your email address
- Sign in with Google
- Sign in with Microsoft
- Sign out from anywhere in the dashboard

## How to sign in with an email link

1. Go to the sign-in page.
2. Type your email address in the field.
3. Select the arrow button to send the link.
4. You will see **Email sent successfully** with the address it went to. Open your inbox and select the link in the message.

If you typed the wrong address, select **Use another email** and start again.

## How to sign in with Google or Microsoft

1. Select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**.
2. A new window opens. Complete the sign-in there.
3. You are returned to the dashboard automatically.

If the connection fails, the screen tells you so and offers to try again.

## How to sign out

1. Select your name and email at the bottom of the left sidebar.
2. Choose **Log out**.

## Tips

- The sign-in link goes to whichever address you typed, so use the address your property account was created with.
- On a shared computer, always sign out when you finish. Signing out also clears your identity from the support tools.

## Limitations and warnings

<Callout type="warn">
  Sign-in links are sent by email. If nothing arrives, check your spam folder before requesting another one.
</Callout>

- There is no self-service sign-up on this screen. Your business account has to exist already.
- Guest accounts cannot be used here, even though they may work elsewhere on OpenBookings.
