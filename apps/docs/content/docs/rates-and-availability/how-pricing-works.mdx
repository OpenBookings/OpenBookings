*Last updated: 10 September 2026*

## Overview

A price is never just a number you typed. It starts from the rate plan's base rate and is moved by adjustments that fire only when their conditions are met. 

## The base rate

Every rate plan has a **base rate** — the starting point for every date. A rate override replaces the base rate on particular dates. 

## The adjustments

On top of the base rate sit adjustments (such as a weekend uplift or a longer-stay discount). They apply **in a fixed order**, and the order changes the result. 

## Checking prices for longer stays

The grid shows the price for a one-night stay by default, and marks any plan with a longer-stay discount waiting with an asterisk (`*`). 

To see what a guest booking longer would be charged, change the stay length list on the toolbar to **Rates for 3 nights** or **Rates for 7 nights**.

## Limitations and warnings

- Booking totals at checkout enforce strict minimum amounts per currency: EUR, USD, and CHF minimum is 50; GBP is 30; SEK and NOK are 300; DKK is 250. Bookings falling below these totals are rejected during checkout.
