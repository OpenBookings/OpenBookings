*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

## Overview

Once a month OpenBookings sends you one invoice for the commission it collected on your bookings in the month before. The commission was already taken from each payment when the guest paid, so the invoice is for your records and for your VAT return. It is marked **Paid** and there is nothing to pay.

## What is on the invoice

The first page has two lines:

- **Commission on bookings**: the commission on the bookings paid that month.
- **Commission refunded on cancellations**: commission returned to you because a booking was refunded, shown as a negative amount with the number of the invoice it relates to.

Below them are the totals and the VAT treatment:

- **Netherlands:** the commission already includes 21% Dutch VAT. The invoice shows the amount without VAT, the VAT, and the total, which is what was collected.
- **Another EU country:** the VAT is reverse charged, so the invoice shows no VAT and a note that you account for it in your own country. This needs a valid VAT number, which we check against VIES.

The pages after it are a specification with one row per booking: the booking reference, when it was paid, the stay dates, the accommodation value (the price of the stay including VAT, excluding tourist tax), the commission and the Stripe payment. No guest names are on it. The same rows come as a CSV file you can open in a spreadsheet.

## When it arrives

After the month closes, and by the 15th at the latest. It is emailed to the billing email in your billing details, with the PDF and the CSV attached. A month with no bookings and no refunds gets no invoice.

If refunds in a month outweigh the commission, you receive a **credit note** instead of an invoice, for the difference. It works the same way and also needs no action.

## Where to download

In the business dashboard, go to **Finance** then **Billing**. Every invoice and credit note is listed there with its PDF and CSV. You can also see the invoice that is coming up: this month's commission and refunds so far, how they will be taxed, and anything that would hold the invoice back.

## Billing details

Invoices are made from your billing details, so complete them before you go live: your legal name, address, country, VAT number and a billing email. A property cannot be published or take bookings until they are in place, and for EU hosts outside the Netherlands the VAT number must be valid. You can change them on the same Billing page. An invoice already sent keeps the details it was issued with.
