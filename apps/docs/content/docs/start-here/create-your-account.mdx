## Overview

The dashboard requires a registered business account. Once your account is provisioned by the onboarding team, you sign in securely without a password using an email link or an existing Google or Microsoft account.

## How to sign in with an email link

1. Go to the sign-in page.
2. Type your email address in the field.
3. Select the arrow button to send the link.
4. You see **Email sent successfully** with the address it went to. 
5. Open your inbox and select the link in the message.

## How to sign in with Google or Microsoft

1. Select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**.
2. A new window opens. Complete the sign-in with your provider.
3. You are returned to the dashboard automatically.

## How to sign out

1. Select your name and email at the bottom of the left sidebar.
2. Choose **Log out**.

This ensures no one else can access your property dashboard on a shared machine.

## Limitations and warnings

- You cannot create a business account directly on the sign-in screen. It must be requested through support.
- Guest accounts are strictly for booking and cannot access the Business Portal. If you attempt to log in with a guest account, you see the error *"This email address is associated with a private account. Please retry with a business email."* and are signed out immediately.
