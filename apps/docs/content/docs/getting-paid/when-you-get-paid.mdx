## Overview

Guest payments follow a strict timeline from the moment the guest checks out to the money arriving in your bank. 

## The timeline

1. A guest's card is held at booking, but the payout cycle starts based on the checkout date.
2. Payouts follow a reconciliation period of exactly 7 calendar days after checkout.
3. Once reconciled, the payout is triggered to your connected payment account and routed to your bank.
