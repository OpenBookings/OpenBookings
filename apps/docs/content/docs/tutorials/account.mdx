## Overview

The dashboard is your central hub for managing your property on OpenBookings. Accessing the dashboard requires a registered business account. Once your account is created, you can sign in securely without a password using an email link or an existing Google or Microsoft account.

### Account Types

There are two primary account types on OpenBookings:
- **Business Accounts:** Used by property managers and owners to access the dashboard.
- **Guest Accounts:** Used by guests to manage their bookings. Guest accounts cannot access the business dashboard.

## Account Creation

To get started with OpenBookings, you must first create a business account.

### Requesting an Account

Currently, business accounts are created through our onboarding team.
1. Contact the OpenBookings support team.
2. Provide your property details and contact information.
3. Wait for an email confirming that your business account has been provisioned.

### Verifying your Identity

Once your account has been provisioned, you will receive an invitation email. 
1. Open the invitation email.
2. Click the verification link to confirm your email address.
3. You will be redirected to the **Business Portal** sign-in screen to authenticate for the first time.

## Signing In

The sign-in screen is headed **Business Portal**. There is no password to remember or reset — sign-in is either a one-time email link or your existing Google or Microsoft account.

### Using an Email Link

1. Go to the sign-in page.
2. Type your email address in the field.
3. Select the arrow button to send the link.
4. You will see **Email sent successfully** with the address it went to. 
5. Open your inbox and select the link in the message.

#### Troubleshooting Email Delivery
<Callout type="warn">
  Sign-in links are sent by email. If nothing arrives, check your spam folder before requesting another one.
</Callout>

### Using Google or Microsoft

1. Select **Continue with Google** or **Continue with Microsoft**.
2. A new window opens. Complete the sign-in with your provider.
3. You are returned to the dashboard automatically.

#### Connection Failures
If the connection fails, the screen will display an error message and offer you a chance to try again. Ensure that you are signing in with the exact Google or Microsoft account linked to your OpenBookings profile.

## Signing Out

For security, especially on shared computers, you should sign out when you are finished. 

### Steps to Sign Out
1. Select your name and email at the bottom of the left sidebar.
2. Choose **Log out**.

#### Why Sign Out?
Signing out ensures that no one else can access your property dashboard and also clears your identity from the support tools.

## Limitations and Warnings

- **No Self-Service Sign-Up:** You cannot create an account directly on the sign-in screen. Your business account must be provisioned beforehand.
- **Guest Accounts:** Guest accounts are strictly for booking and cannot be used to log into the Business Portal. If you attempt to log in with a guest account, you will be shown an error and signed out immediately.
