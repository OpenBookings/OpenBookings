---
title: Taking a room out of service
description: Hold rooms back for maintenance or owner use without disturbing your rates.
---

## Overview

A room needs redecorating, a radiator has failed, or an owner is staying. You want that room unavailable for a period, everything else untouched, and a record of why.

## The approach

Use **blocked units**, not an availability override. Blocking removes rooms from the pool while letting the calculation keep running, so the number stays correct as new bookings arrive. An override freezes at whatever you typed and does not adjust. See [Availability](/docs/explanation/availability).

## Steps

1. Open [Rates & Availability](/docs/reference/rates-and-availability) and set the date range so the whole period is visible.
2. Select **Edit availability** on the toolbar.
3. Choose the **Room type** the room belongs to.
4. Set **From** and **To** to cover the full period — including the day work finishes, if the room will not be sellable that night.
5. In **Blocked units**, enter how many rooms are out of service. For one room, enter `1`. If rooms are already blocked for another reason, enter the total.
6. Leave **Availability override** and **Capacity override** empty.
7. In **Note**, write what is happening and when it ends — for example *Room 12 redecorating, back 14 March*.
8. Select **Apply to range**.

## Check it worked

Select one of the dates in the **Available rooms** row. The panel should show your blocked figure under **How this number is reached**, the calculated baseline reduced by that amount, and **Manual override** reading *Not set*.

Your note and your name appear under **Last change**, so whoever looks next knows why.

## Putting the room back

Repeat the steps for the same room type and dates, setting **Blocked units** back to `0` — or to whatever is still legitimately blocked. Update the note.

<Callout type="warn">
  Blocked units do not expire on their own. If maintenance finishes early, nothing releases the rooms until you do. Put a reminder in your diary for the end date.
</Callout>

## Tips

- Block the whole expected period at once rather than a day at a time, and shorten it later if work finishes early. Under-blocking risks selling a room that is not ready.
- Blocking affects every rate plan on that room type, which is what you want here — the rooms genuinely do not exist for that period.

## Limitations and warnings

- The dashboard tracks room types, not individual rooms. Blocking one unit removes *a* room from the pool; it does not reserve the specific room number.
- If the room type already has bookings that would now exceed what is left, blocking does not cancel them. Check the **Booked** figure before blocking heavily.
