## Overview

Sign-in issues are generally caused by using the wrong type of account or a failed login link.

## How to fix private account errors

If you see *"This email address is associated with a private account. Please retry with a business email."* and are signed out immediately, you are trying to log in using a guest account. Guest accounts are for booking trips and cannot access the Business Portal. You must sign in using the email address provisioned during onboarding.

## How to fix failed links

If you request a magic link and see *"Hmmm... try again."*, the link request failed. Wait 10 minutes and try again.
