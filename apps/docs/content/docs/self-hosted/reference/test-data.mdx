to the day you run it. `testing/README.md` has the same information, next to the files.

## Hotels

All three belong to **Lantern Hotels B.V.**, a Dutch organisation with a complete billing profile
and the inclusive-rates confirmation.

| Hotel | City | State | Cancellation tier | Rooms (units) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Canal Lantern Hotel | Amsterdam | live | Flex: free until 24 h before | Classic Double (8), Canal View Double (4), Family Room (2) |
| Dom Garden Suites | Utrecht | live | Standard: free until 7 days before | Garden Studio (6), Dom View Suite (2) |
| Singel Loft House | Amsterdam | draft | Standard | Loft (3). Legal details missing, so not ready. |

On the rates grid, relative to today:

- **Classic Double:** Flexible €165 and Non-refundable €149; +€30 on Fridays and Saturdays (Flexible).
- **Family Room:** bed and breakfast, €260, 2-night minimum.
- **Canal View Double:** one unit out of service, days +7 to +10.
- **Garden Studio:** 3-night minimum, days +28 to +34.
- **Dom View Suite:** closed, days +60 to +62.
- **Tourist tax:** Amsterdam 12.5% of the room price before 9% VAT; Utrecht €3.50 per adult per night.
