## Overview

If a payout has not reached your bank account, check the timeline to ensure the reconciliation period has passed.

## How to verify the timeline

Payouts are only released after a strict 7-day calendar reconciliation period counting from the guest's **checkout** date, not the booking date. If 7 days have not yet passed since checkout, the funds are still being held by the platform.

If 7 days have passed, verify that your payment account remains in good standing and is not requesting further KYC documentation.
