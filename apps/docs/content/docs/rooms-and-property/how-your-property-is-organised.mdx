## Overview

Everything in the dashboard hangs off three layers. Getting them straight explains why one change ripples further than you expected.

## Your property

The top level. One business account manages one property. Its name, description, photos, house rules and address are set once during setup.

## Room types

A room type is a group of interchangeable rooms — *Deluxe Double*, *Family Room*. What matters is the **number of units** it has: how many physical rooms of that kind you can sell.

Availability is counted per room type, not per individual room. The dashboard does not track which specific room a guest is in.

## Rate plans

A rate plan is one *way of selling* a room type. The same rooms might be sold as a flexible rate and as a cheaper non-refundable rate. Each plan carries its own base rate, terms, and stay limits.

## The shared pool

> **Rate plans on the same room type all draw from the same pool of rooms.**

Adding a rate plan gives guests another way to book. It does not give you another room to sell. This explains behaviour that otherwise looks like a bug:

- A room type sells out, and *every* one of its rate plans goes unsellable at once, because the shared pool is empty.
- Closing one rate plan leaves the others still selling, because closures apply to a plan, not to the rooms.
- Blocking rooms affects every plan on that room type.

When you want something off sale, ask: **the rooms, or one way of selling them?**
- Rooms unavailable — change availability.
- One offer withdrawn — set a closure.

## Limitations and warnings

- Each business account works with the property it owns. You do not see other properties, and they do not see yours.
