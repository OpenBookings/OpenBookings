## Overview

Every reservation carries a status describing where the guest is in their stay. The statuses drive the lists and counters you see on the Overview and Reservations screens.

## The statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | The booking needs a decision or is not yet confirmed. |
| **Confirmed** | Booked and expected. |
| **Checked in** | The guest is on the property. |
| **Checked out** | The stay is finished. |

The colours follow the guest's journey: booked, on the property, needs a decision, done.

## The four lists

The same reservations are grouped four ways, by date and status rather than by status alone:

| List | What lands in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Upcoming** | Arriving on a future date. |
| **Check-ins** | Arriving today. |
| **Check-outs** | Leaving today. |
| **In-house** | Currently checked in. |

A reservation can appear in more than one list — someone arriving today is in **Check-ins**, and once they arrive, also in **In-house**.

<Callout type="info">
  On the Overview screen, the **In-house** card counts *guests*, while the other three count *reservations*. A family of four adds one to Check-ins but four to In-house.
</Callout>

## Limitations and warnings

<Callout type="warn">
  Statuses cannot be changed from the dashboard yet. Checking a guest in or out, marking a no-show and cancelling are not available.
</Callout>
