## Overview

The dashboard has the same frame on every screen: a sidebar down the left for moving between sections, and a bar across the top showing where you are with a search box on the right. Learning these two makes everything else quicker to find.

## The sidebar

The top of the sidebar carries your property name. Below it are the main sections:

| Item | What is behind it |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | Today's arrivals, departures and in-house guests. |
| **Bookings** | Opens to reveal **Reservations**, **Messages** and **Reviews**. |
| **Property** | Opens to reveal **R&A** (rates and availability), **Property** and **Rooms**. |
| **Finance** | Payouts and money matters. |
| **Analytics** | Performance reporting. |

**Bookings** and **Property** are groups — select one to expand it and reveal the pages inside.

At the bottom of the sidebar are four more items:

- **Notifications** — recent activity across your property, shown in a window. A dot appears next to it when there is something you have not seen.
- **What's New** — the latest changes to the dashboard, newest first. This also shows a dot for anything unread.
- **Support** — help with your account.
- **Documentation** — opens this guide in a new tab.

Right at the bottom is your name and email. Select it for **Profile**, **Security** and **Log out**.

### Collapsing the sidebar

Select the control next to your property name to shrink the sidebar to a narrow strip of icons, giving screens like the rates grid more room. Select it again to bring the labels back.

## The top bar

The left of the top bar shows a trail of where you are, for example *Bookings → Reservations*. Earlier parts of the trail are links, so you can step back up a level.

## Search and the command menu

On the right of the top bar is a **Search...** box. Select it, or press **⌘K** (**Ctrl+K** on Windows), to open the command menu. From here you can:

- Type two or more characters to find a reservation by its number or by guest name
- Jump straight to any section — start typing the name of the page you want
- Reach account settings, switch theme, or log out

The menu also lists **New Reservation**, **Check In Guest** and **Check Out Guest**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Several command menu entries are placeholders. **New Reservation**, **Check In Guest**, **Check Out Guest**, **Account Settings**, **Switch Property**, **Change Theme**, **Log Out**, **Search Help Center**, **Send Feedback** and **Contact Support** currently just close the menu without doing anything. The entries under **Navigation** do work.
</Callout>

## Tips

- **⌘K** works from any screen, so it is usually the fastest way to move around.
- The command menu matches on related words as well as exact names — searching for "pricing" or "calendar" finds Rates & Availability.

## Limitations and warnings

- Notifications and What's New are read-only. There is nothing to action from them and you cannot dismiss individual entries.
- **Switch Property** is listed in the command menu but is not wired up. Each account works with one property.
