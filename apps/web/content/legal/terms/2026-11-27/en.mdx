**2.3** You pay the property directly. Your payment is processed by Stripe on the property's behalf, and the charge on your bank or card statement shows the property's name. We never hold or control your money.

**2.4** We work on behalf of the property, not on your behalf. We take bookings, send confirmations, apply the property's cancellation policy and pass on messages. The property pays us a commission. You pay us nothing.

**2.5** Your consumer rights for the stay itself (for example, if the room is not as described) are rights against the property. We are responsible for the platform: that it works as these Terms describe. [Section 9](#problems-during-your-stay) explains how we help when something goes wrong.

<Section id="cancellations" number="8" title="Cancellations, changes and refunds">

**8.1 Your booking's cancellation policy.** Every rate has a cancellation policy, which the property chooses from our standard set. You see it before you book and in your confirmation. That policy decides whether you get money back and how much. The policy that applied when you booked stays in force, even if the property changes it later.

**8.2 How to cancel.** Cancel in your account, or email us at [support@openbookings.co](mailto:support@openbookings.co). In your account, the cancellation counts from the moment you confirm it. A cancellation sent to support is processed by a person, and counts from when we process it, not from when you sent it. To lock in the time of your cancellation, cancel in your account. Deadlines are counted in the property's local time.

If you agree a cancellation or change directly with the property, that agreement is between you and the property. We can only process a refund through OpenBookings once the property has recorded it with us.

**8.3 Changes.** To change your dates or room, contact us or the property. A change is only confirmed once the property agrees, and it may change the price.

**8.4 No-shows.** If you don't arrive and haven't cancelled, you pay the full price and get nothing back, tourist tax included. This applies on every rate. If you'll arrive late, tell the property.

**8.5 Refunds.** Refunds are paid by the property to your original payment method. They usually arrive within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. We email you when a cancellation is processed, and your booking page shows each refund and its status.

**8.6 Checking our decisions.** We apply cancellation policies automatically. If you think we applied a policy incorrectly, contact support and ask for a review. A person will look at it and reply within 5 business days.

**8.7 Exceptional circumstances.** You get a full refund of everything you paid, on any rate, if for your dates a government measure bans travel to or a stay at the place where the property is, or bans residents of your country from entering that country, or the authorities have declared a disaster or emergency there that makes the stay impossible or unsafe. Ask us before your check-in time and send reasonable evidence. A person at OpenBookings reviews each request.

For other personal circumstances, such as illness, you can ask for an exception. We pass your request to the property, and the property decides. This doesn't affect any rights you have under the law.

**8.8 If the property cancels.** If the property cancels your booking, you get a full refund of everything you paid, paid by the property. Where we can, we help you find somewhere else to stay.

</Section>

**9.3** For other problems, such as disappointment with the room, personal circumstances or mistakes in your booking, we help you and the property communicate. We don't decide these, and we don't issue refunds for them.
