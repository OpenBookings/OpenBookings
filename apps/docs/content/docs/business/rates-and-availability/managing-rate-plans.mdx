*Last updated: 16 November 2026*

## Overview

A rate plan gives guests a specific way to book your rooms. 

## How to see why a price is what it is

1. Select the price cell in the grid.
2. The panel opens on **Price breakdown**.
3. The top line is the base rate, or the rate override if one applies.
4. Below it, every adjustment on the plan is listed. Ones that fired show their amount; ones that did not are dimmed and marked *not applied*. See [How pricing works](/business/rates-and-availability/how-pricing-works) for how they combine.
5. The bottom line is the final price per night.

## How to add a rate plan

1. Select **Add rate plan** on the toolbar.
2. Choose the **Room type** the plan belongs to.
3. Give it a **Name** guests understand, such as *Non-refundable*.
4. Set the **Base rate**. The currency is shown on the field label.
5. Set **Minimum stay** and, if you want one, **Maximum stay**.
6. Tick or clear **Refundable**. A refundable plan follows your property's cancellation policy. A non-refundable plan must be at least 10% cheaper than a refundable plan on the same room.
7. Select **Create rate plan**.

## Limitations and warnings

- A new rate plan sells the room type's existing rooms. It does not add units to your property.
- There is no free-text cancellation policy per plan. A plan is either refundable, under your property's cancellation policy, or non-refundable.
- To change a plan's base rate, open the plan in the detail panel and select **Edit base rate**. To add, edit or switch off price adjustments, select **Pricing rules** in the same panel.
- To change a plan's name, description, meals, extras or refundability, or to switch it off, open the room in **Listings → Rooms** and select the rate. A change applies to new bookings only.
- A plan can't be deleted. You can archive it from the same place, unless it has upcoming bookings; switch it off instead to stop new bookings.
