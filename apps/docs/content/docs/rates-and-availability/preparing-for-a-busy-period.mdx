*Last updated: 10 September 2026*

## Overview

Before a period you expect to sell out, it pays to check that nothing is quietly closed and your stay rules are correct.

## How to run an inventory check

1. Open Rates & Availability and step forward to the busy period.
2. Select **Filter** and tick **Show only with issues**. The grid now shows only room types with something closed, restricted or sold out.
3. For each room type, expand it and check the bars:
   - **Closed (Red)**: Select the bar to see who closed it. Reopen if it should be selling.
   - **Restricted (Amber)**: Check if the minimum stay rule is still what you want.
   - **Sold out (Grey striped)**: Check the blocked figure to ensure you haven't over-blocked.
4. Switch the stay length list to **Rates for 3 nights**. If prices drop noticeably, a longer-stay discount is firing, which you may not want during high demand.
5. Select the filter icon next to **Filter** to clear the issues view.

## Limitations and warnings

- Longer-stay discounts and other price adjustments cannot be turned off from the dashboard. If a discount is firing when you would rather it did not, the only lever available is the stay restrictions on the plan.
