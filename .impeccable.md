# Impeccable — design context

The visual system (tokens, Glass vs Console modes, the spectrum) lives in
[`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md). This file holds what code can't tell
you: who each surface is for and how it should feel.

## Design Context

### Users

- **Guests** (`apps/web`): travellers booking boutique and larger, professionally run
  hotels across the EU (go-to-market: Amsterdam, Utrecht). They browse and decide on
  Glass surfaces, then come back after booking to check their stay, message the
  property, or cancel.
- **Hosts** (`apps/business`): hotel staff working in the Console dashboard.

### Brand Personality

Transparent, warm, quietly proud. Never loud. The spectrum enters as refracted
light (hairlines, halos, dots), never as paint (DESIGN_SYSTEM.md §3.5).

**After booking** (`/bookings/[id]`, the account area): the guest should feel

1. **Excited, first of all.** They booked a trip; the page is a small party for it.
   The stay (the place, the dates, the countdown) leads.
2. **Reassured.** At a glance: the booking came through and everything is as intended.
3. **In control, when they look for it.** Cancelling, messaging, and what each would
   cost are easy to find and unambiguous, but they don't compete with the trip.

### Aesthetic Direction

- Dark Glass over the property's own imagery (blurred backdrop), Gloock display over
  Libre Franklin.
- One lead moment per view. Admin detail (policy rules, payment) is quiet and
  secondary; colour marks state, never decorates.
- Anti-references: dense dashboards of coloured bars; every element in its own card;
  warning colours on a happy page; generic "order status" admin screens.

### Design Principles

1. **Celebrate the trip, then reassure, then empower.** That's the order of visual weight.
2. **One thing glows.** Only the current state gets colour; past and future are neutral.
3. **Say it in a sentence.** A plain-language line beats a chart or a legend.
4. **Actions are calm.** Cancel is reachable but never the loudest thing on the page.
5. **Split on desktop, stack on phones.** Stay on the left, "right now" status on the right.
