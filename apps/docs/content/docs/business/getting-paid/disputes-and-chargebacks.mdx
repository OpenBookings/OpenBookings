## Overview

A dispute (or chargeback) occurs when a guest challenges a payment with their bank. 

## How we handle chargebacks

The platform absorbs disputes. Your host payouts are strictly payout-only; you do not process cards directly and do not deal with chargebacks or their associated financial penalties.

If a guest uses dispute vocabulary in a message (e.g. threatening a chargeback), the support bot automatically escalates the conversation to human support for a dedicated review.
