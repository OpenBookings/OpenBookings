## Overview

**Finance** is where invoicing, payments, payouts and financial reporting will live. It is not built yet: opening it shows a **Coming soon** message.

## What you can do here

Nothing yet. The page is a placeholder.

## How you get paid today

Payments and payouts run through the Stripe account you connected during setup. That connection is what allows you to take bookings at all — until Stripe approved your account, the dashboard stayed locked.

Payouts reach the bank account held on that Stripe account. Because the Finance page is not built, payout details, statements and transaction history are not visible in the OpenBookings dashboard.

See [Setting up your property](/docs/getting-started/setting-up-your-property) for how that connection was made.

## Limitations and warnings

<Callout type="warn">
  There is currently no way to see your payouts, invoices, transactions, taxes or refunds from the dashboard, and no way to export financial records.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Notifications sometimes mention payouts. Those entries are examples, not live payout information.
</Callout>
