*Last updated: 25 November 2026*

## Overview

Every OpenBookings booking, change and cancellation goes to SiteMinder, which passes it on to your PMS and takes the room off sale on your other channels. Each booking shows whether it arrived.

## What is sent

| Event on OpenBookings | Sent to SiteMinder as |
| --- | --- |
| New booking | A new booking |
| Some rooms of a booking cancelled | A change |
| Early departure | A change, with the new dates |
| Whole booking cancelled | A cancellation |

Check-in, no-show and refunds are not sent.

Each booking includes:

- **Booking reference** without dashes. `OB-B7B0-2A1C` arrives as `OBB7B02A1C`. It never changes.
- **First and last name** of the person who booked and of the guest.
- **Rooms, dates and rate plans.**
- **Guest counts**: adults and children. Infants under 2 aren't counted as guests and are added as a note, for example *1 infant under 2, not in the guest counts.*
- **Price per night**, including VAT, as the guest paid it.
- **Tourist tax**, as city tax.
- **Our commission**.
- **Cots and extra beds** as a note with the amount to pay at the hotel, for example *1 cot, payable at the hotel: EUR 15.00.*
- The note *Paid in full online via OpenBookings. Cots and extra beds are payable at the hotel.*

Not sent: the guest's email, phone or address, card details, and the guest's notes. Read those in the booking panel in OpenBookings.

The guest has already paid OpenBookings. Don't charge them again at the hotel, except for cots and extra beds. Your payout works as usual. See [When you get paid](/business/getting-paid/when-you-get-paid).

## Delivery status

Open a booking in **Bookings → Reservations**. At the end of the panel, the **SiteMinder** section shows where it stands:

| Status | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sent** and a time | SiteMinder confirmed it at that time. | Nothing. |
| **Sending** | On its way. This usually takes under a minute, and at most about 5 minutes. | Nothing. |
| **Retrying** | SiteMinder didn't answer. We keep trying for up to 30 minutes. | Nothing yet. |
| **Not delivered** | SiteMinder didn't accept it, kept not answering, or hasn't confirmed it within 30 minutes. We email you when this happens. | Add it by hand (see below). |
| **Not sent** | Made before you connected, and you chose to add these bookings by hand. | Nothing. |

A change or cancellation is sent the same way, and the status shows how the latest one went.

## When a booking is not delivered

The top of the booking panel says what didn't arrive and what to do:

| What didn't arrive | The panel says |
| --- | --- |
| A new booking | *Not in SiteMinder yet. Add it in SiteMinder or your PMS by hand, or check the connection.* |
| A cancellation | *The cancellation didn't reach SiteMinder. Cancel it in SiteMinder or your PMS by hand, or check the connection.* |
| A change (rooms cancelled, an early departure, new dates) | *The latest change didn't reach SiteMinder. Update it in SiteMinder or your PMS by hand, or check the connection.* |
| A booking reopened after SiteMinder got its cancellation | *This booking was reopened after SiteMinder received its cancellation. Add it again in SiteMinder or your PMS by hand.* SiteMinder can't take a cancellation back. |

The Channel manager page counts the bookings that need attention.

<Callout type="warn">
  A booking that is **Not delivered** is not in SiteMinder, so your other channels may still sell that room. Add it by hand as soon as you see it.
</Callout>

Nothing is cancelled or refunded because of a delivery problem. If the room really was sold twice, contact support.

## The email when a booking doesn't arrive

When something can't be delivered, we also email the hotel's team. The subject says what didn't arrive:

- *Booking OB-… didn't reach SiteMinder*
- *Change to booking OB-… didn't reach SiteMinder*
- *Cancellation of booking OB-… didn't reach SiteMinder*
- *Reopened booking OB-… isn't in SiteMinder*

It names the booking and the dates, what to do by hand, and what SiteMinder said if it gave a reason. You get one email each time a booking becomes **Not delivered**.

## Bookings made before you connect

When the hotel has upcoming bookings (bookings that haven't checked out yet), the connect screen shows a tick box: **Send your N upcoming bookings to SiteMinder**. It is ticked by default.

- **Ticked:** those bookings go to SiteMinder as new bookings, and their changes and cancellations follow.
- **Unticked:** use this if you've already added them to SiteMinder or your PMS by hand. They are marked **Not sent** and stay that way. Their later changes and cancellations aren't sent either, so keep them up to date by hand.

## Limitations and warnings

- SiteMinder accepts every booking we send. It doesn't check whether the room is still free.
- SiteMinder can't tell us whether a booking reached your PMS. **Sent** means SiteMinder has it.
- Without Auto-Replenishment in SiteMinder, a cancellation doesn't put the room back on sale on your other channels. See [Settings in SiteMinder](/business/channel-managers/settings-in-siteminder).
