*Last updated: 16 November 2026*

## Overview

You handle cancellations, no-shows and refunds from the details panel of the reservation. The panel only shows the actions that are possible for that booking right now. Hover over an icon to see its name.

## The actions

| Action | When it is available | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Check in** | From 24 hours before check-in time until the stay is over. | The booking is marked as checked in. |
| **Cancel booking** | Until the stay is over, for a confirmed booking. | The booking is cancelled and the guest gets a **full refund**, whatever your cancellation policy. A booking with several rooms lets you cancel one room or all of them. |
| **Mark as no-show** | Once your check-in window on the arrival day has closed, until 24 hours after check-out. | The guest gets nothing back, tourist tax included. |
| **Guest left early** | After check-in time, until the stay is over. | You pick the last night the guest stayed. Of the unused nights, the next 7 are kept and any after that are refunded, on every cancellation tier. Tourist tax for every unused night is refunded. |
| **Refund booking** | Any time money is left to refund, including after a cancellation or no-show. | A goodwill refund of an amount you choose, up to what is left on the booking. |
| **Retry refund** | When a refund didn't go through. | OpenBookings tries the failed refund again. |

Refunds go back to the guest's card through your Stripe account. Commission on the refunded part is returned to you, see [How commission works](/business/getting-paid/how-commission-works).

## Limitations and warnings

<Callout type="warn">
  Each action asks you to confirm first, and can't be undone from the dashboard. Changing a booking's dates or rooms is not available yet.
</Callout>
