## Overview

The first time you sign in, you are taken through a setup process instead of the dashboard. It collects what OpenBookings needs to show your property to guests, invoice correctly, and pay you.

## How to provide your property details

1. Enter your **Property name**. This is required and is the name guests see.
2. Optionally add a **Tagline**.
3. Write a **Description** covering the location and what you offer.
4. Add **Property photos**. Select the upload area to browse, or drag files onto it. 
5. Optionally add your **House rules**.
6. Select the button at the bottom right to continue.

## How to set your location

1. Start typing your address in **Search address**. 
2. Choose the matching suggestion to drop a map pin.
3. Check the **Street address**, **City**, **Postal code** and **Country** fields, and correct anything that came through wrong.
4. Continue to the next step.

## How to sign the agreement

1. Fill in **Legal Company Name**, **Full Name**, **Role / Title** and **VAT Number**. 
2. Add your **CoC Number** if you have one.
3. Select **Sign the Partner Agreement**.
4. Read the confirmation window, then select **Sign Document**.
5. The button changes to **Signed & Sent**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Signing is an irreversible action. By clicking **Sign Document**, your IP address is recorded permanently as part of the legally binding record. The legal detail fields are then locked and cannot be edited.
</Callout>

*(Note: While some documentation may refer to a Host Agreement, the UI and binding document is the Partner Agreement.)*

## How to connect payouts

1. Select the button to continue. You are taken to the payment processor's secure onboarding.
2. Complete the details asked for and return to OpenBookings.
3. The page updates automatically. If it says **Your account is under review**, wait 5 minutes and refresh. 

Once approved, you are moved to the dashboard.

## Limitations and warnings

- Setup must be completed in order. 
- You cannot skip ahead to payouts before the earlier steps are done.
- Once setup is complete, the setup flow is closed permanently. 
- You cannot accept bookings until your payment account is approved.
