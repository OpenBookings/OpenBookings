title: Troubleshooting
description: What to check when a room isn't on sale, a price looks wrong or a booking didn't reach SiteMinder.
---

*Last updated: 25 November 2026*

## Overview

Most problems come from a room or rate plan that isn't linked in SiteMinder, or from a SiteMinder setting that doesn't match how OpenBookings works. Start with the Channel manager page (**About → CM**): it shows the status, how many rooms are linked and when the last update arrived.

## The page stays on Waiting for SiteMinder

SiteMinder hasn't sent anything yet.

1. In SiteMinder, check that the OpenBookings channel is added with the **Hotel code** from the Channel manager page, exactly as shown.
2. Check that at least one room and rate plan are linked.
3. Run a full refresh of OpenBookings in SiteMinder.

## A room isn't on sale

The grid marks it *Not linked in SiteMinder yet · not on sale*. A room goes on sale once SiteMinder has sent it both a price and availability.

1. In SiteMinder, check that the room and its rate plans are linked to OpenBookings.
2. Check that SiteMinder has a price and availability for those nights.
3. If you made the room or rate plan inactive and active again in OpenBookings, link it again in SiteMinder.
4. Run a full refresh of OpenBookings in SiteMinder.

If the room is linked but a date can't be booked, check the restrictions for that night. See [A guest can't book a date](/business/somethings-not-right/a-guest-cant-book-a-date).

## A price is different from SiteMinder

1. Check whether the difference is **tourist tax**. OpenBookings adds it on top of SiteMinder's price. Don't include it in SiteMinder.
2. Check the number of guests. SiteMinder's price is for the guests included in the room. Extra adults and children pay SiteMinder's extra amounts.
3. Check that the price in SiteMinder **includes VAT**.
4. Check **Last update from SiteMinder** on the Channel manager page. If it's old, SiteMinder may not have sent the change yet.

## OpenBookings shows one room fewer than SiteMinder

This is expected for a short time after a booking. We keep counting our own booking until SiteMinder sends a newer number, for up to 30 minutes. See [How rooms to sell are counted](/business/channel-managers/working-while-connected#how-rooms-to-sell-are-counted).

## A booking is Not delivered

Add it in SiteMinder or your PMS by hand, as the booking panel says. See [When a booking is not delivered](/business/channel-managers/bookings-and-delivery#when-a-booking-is-not-delivered). If several bookings are **Not delivered** at once, check that the connection is still active in SiteMinder, then contact support.

## A cancelled room didn't come back on my other channels

SiteMinder only puts a room back on sale after a cancellation when **Auto-Replenishment** is on. Turn it on in SiteMinder, and put the room back on sale by hand for cancellations that already happened.

## SiteMinder shows an error for an update

- *Invalid included occupancy*: you're using occupancy-based pricing or Included Occupancy. Use one price per night with extra-adult and extra-child amounts. See [Settings in SiteMinder](/business/channel-managers/settings-in-siteminder#not-supported).
- An error about a room or rate plan that wasn't found: it isn't active in OpenBookings, or it was removed. Check **About → Rooms**.

## Still stuck

Contact support with the hotel code, the room and the dates involved, and the booking reference if it's about a booking.
