*Last updated: 10 November 2026*

## Overview

**Reviews** sits under Bookings in the sidebar. It lists the reviews guests wrote about your properties, and the number next to it shows how many are waiting for a reply.

Only guests who booked through OpenBookings and stayed can review. We email them a day after check-out. A review shows a score from 1 to 10, the guest's first name and country, and the month of the stay, never the exact dates.

## The page

- **The summary** shows the average guests see on your property page (from 3 reviews, over the last 24 months), how the scores spread over Terrific, Great, Good, Okay and Bad, and how many reviews wait for a reply.
- **To reply** lists the reviews you haven't handled yet. **All** lists every published review. **Being checked** lists reviews our automated check held back, for example because they contain a link or a phone number. A person at OpenBookings looks at those before they're shown.
- Click a review to read it. **View reservation** opens the booking it belongs to.

## Replying

Every reply starts with one question: **publicly** or **privately**?

- **Publicly** posts your reply under the review on your property page, signed with the property name. Your own name is never shown. The guest gets an email with your reply and can answer it once, publicly. You then get the last word, after which the thread closes and you carry on in Messages.
- **Privately** sends a message to the guest in the booking's conversation in [Messages](/business/messages), with the review attached. Nothing is shown on your property page.
- **No reply needed** takes the review off **To reply** without sending anything. You can undo it, or still reply later. If the guest answers you after that, the review needs a reply again.

Replies go through the same automated check as reviews. A reply with a link or contact details waits for a person before it's shown.

You can edit your public replies at any time; an edited reply shows "Edited". You can delete your latest reply in a thread. You can't edit or remove a guest's review. If a review breaks the rules, use **Report** to open a support ticket about it.
