*Last updated: 10 September 2026*

## Overview

When a guest tells you they cannot complete a booking, the cause is either a limitation on their card, a configuration problem with your property, or a temporary lock on the room.

## How to check for room holds

When a guest begins checkout, the room is held for 30 minutes (with a 60-second margin). If the guest closes their browser before finishing, or a different guest is looking at the same room, the date appears unavailable until that hold expires.

Wait 31 minutes and check again. 

## How to check for card limits

If the guest sees *"That card has hit a spending or transaction limit set by your bank"*, their card issuer has declined the charge for velocity or spending limits. The guest must retry using a different card or contact their bank. You cannot override this.

## How to check for configuration errors

If the guest receives a non-retryable checkout error, your property setup is incomplete. The most common cause is missing the payouts connection. The guest cannot retry their way out of this. You must complete your payment account setup, or contact support if the onboarding flow is already locked.
