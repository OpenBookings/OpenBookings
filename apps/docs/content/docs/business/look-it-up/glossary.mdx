---
title: Glossary
description: What specific OpenBookings terms mean.
---

*Last updated: 10 September 2026*

- **Availability** — The number of rooms left to sell of a given room type.
- **Base rate** — The starting price of a rate plan on any given date, before modifiers apply.
- **Blocked unit** — A room taken out of the shared pool, usually for maintenance.
- **Capacity** — The total physical number of rooms of a specific type.
- **Closure** — A restriction preventing a specific rate plan from being booked on a date.
- **Modifier** — A pricing rule (like a weekend uplift) that alters the base rate.
- **Property** — The overarching business entity. 
- **Rate plan** — A specific set of conditions and pricing to sell a room type (e.g. Non-refundable).
- **Restriction** — A condition a booking must satisfy, like a minimum stay.
- **Room type** — A category grouping identical physical rooms together (e.g. Double Deluxe).
