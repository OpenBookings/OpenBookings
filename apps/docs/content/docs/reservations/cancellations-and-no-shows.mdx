## Overview

You handle cancellations and no-shows from the details panel of the reservation.

## How to cancel or mark a no-show

The detail panel shows buttons for **Edit details**, **Mark no-show** and **Request Cancellation**.

## Limitations and warnings

<Callout type="warn">
  These buttons are not yet connected. Selecting them does nothing. Creating, changing, cancelling and checking guests in or out are not available in the dashboard yet. Statuses cannot be changed manually today.
</Callout>
