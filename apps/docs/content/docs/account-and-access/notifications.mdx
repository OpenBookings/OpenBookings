## Overview

The dashboard includes a system status indicator that reports on system health, upcoming maintenance, and new messages.

## The status bar

The status bar updates its display automatically. 

<Callout type="info">
  System status and updates are cached for 15 minutes to preserve performance. This means a notification might disappear or reappear slightly out of sync if you move between devices within that window.
</Callout>
