## Overview

A dispute (or chargeback) occurs when a guest challenges a payment with their bank.

## Who handles a dispute

Guest payments are made directly to your own Stripe account, so a dispute is raised against your account and you are the one who responds to it.

- Stripe notifies you by email and shows the dispute in your Stripe Dashboard, with a deadline for your response.
- You submit your evidence (the booking confirmation, your cancellation policy, messages with the guest) in the Stripe Dashboard.
- If the dispute is lost, the disputed amount and Stripe's dispute fee are taken from your Stripe balance.

OpenBookings does not decide disputes and cannot respond to them for you.

## What OpenBookings does

- If a dispute is lost, we return our commission on that booking to your Stripe account. If the booking did not happen, we do not earn.
- If a guest uses dispute vocabulary in a message (for example, threatening a chargeback), the support bot escalates the conversation to human support.
