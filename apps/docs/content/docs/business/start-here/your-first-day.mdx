## Overview

**Overview** is the landing screen of the dashboard. It answers three questions immediately: who is leaving today, who is arriving today, and how many guests are currently on the property.

## The four counters

Across the top are four counters calculated from today's date:

- **C/O's Today** — Reservations checking out today.
- **C/I's Today** — Reservations checking in today.
- **In-house** — Guests currently staying. This counts people, not reservations.
- **Upcoming** — Reservations arriving on a future date.

## The reservation lists

Below the counters are four tabs matching the counter categories. Select a tab to see those reservations in a table. The table shows the reservation number, guest, room, dates, number of guests, status and total.

When a list is empty it says so plainly, for example *No check-ins today*.

## Limitations and warnings

- The reservations shown on the Overview screen are sample data, not your property's real bookings. Treat this screen as a preview until live reservations are connected.
- Rows on the Overview screen cannot be selected to open a detail panel. Use the [Reservations](/business/reservations/finding-a-booking) page for that.
