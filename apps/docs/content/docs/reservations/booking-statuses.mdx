## Overview

Every reservation carries a status describing where the guest is in their stay. 

## The statuses

- **Pending** — The booking needs a decision or is not yet confirmed.
- **Confirmed** — Booked and expected.
- **Checked in** — The guest is on the property.
- **Checked out** — The stay is finished.

## How statuses feed lists

Reservations are grouped into four lists based on their status and date:

- **Upcoming** — Arriving on a future date.
- **Check-ins** — Arriving today.
- **Check-outs** — Leaving today.
- **In-house** — Currently checked in.

A reservation appears in more than one list if applicable — someone arriving today is in **Check-ins**, and once they arrive, also in **In-house**.
