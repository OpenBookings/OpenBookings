## Overview

Selecting a row in the [Reservations](/docs/reservations/finding-a-booking) table opens a details panel containing the full record of the stay.

## Reading the details panel

The panel opens from the right on a large screen, and from the bottom on a smaller one. It shows:

- **Guest** — name and party size.
- **Stay** — room, check-in date, check-out date and number of nights.
- **Payment** — what the guest pays for each night, and the total.

The nightly rows in the Payment section always add up to the total shown beneath them. Amounts are shown in euros.

## How to share a link to one reservation

Open the reservation, then copy the address from your browser. Sending it to a colleague opens the exact same panel for them.
