- **Eligibility.** A Listing is shown when it is published, lies within 250 km of the place the Guest searched for, and has a room available that suits the Guest's dates and party.
- **Default order ("Recommended").** Eligible Listings are ordered by the total price of their cheapest suitable room for the Guest's search, lowest first.

<Section id="bookings" number="3" title="Bookings, Cancellations and No-Shows">

### 3.1 Booking Confirmation

A Booking is confirmed when the Guest's payment into the Host's Stripe Account succeeds. OpenBookings notifies the Host of each confirmed Booking by email and shows it in the business portal.

### 3.2 Host Acceptance

By maintaining active Listings with available inventory, the Host grants standing acceptance of Bookings that match the published rates and availability. The Host may not unilaterally cancel a confirmed Booking except in the limited circumstances described in Section 3.4.

### 3.3 Cancellation Policy

Cancellations are governed by Annex B. Each Booking is subject to the Cancellation Policy Tier, and to whether the rate is Non-refundable, as shown to the Guest at the moment of booking. A change to the Host's Tier or rates applies only to Bookings made after the change.

OpenBookings applies the Cancellation Policy on the Host's behalf. When a Guest cancels through the Platform, OpenBookings calculates the refund under Annex B and executes it straight away, without further approval from the Host (see Section 5.1).

### 3.4 Host-Initiated Cancellations

Host-initiated cancellation of a confirmed Booking is a material breach of this Agreement, except where a Force Majeure Event (as defined in Section 11.10) prevents the Host from honouring the Booking. Where the Host cancels a Booking:

- The Guest receives a full refund of the total price paid, from the Host's Stripe Account, regardless of the Cancellation Policy Tier;
- OpenBookings returns its full Commission on the Booking in accordance with Section 4.3;
- The cancellation is recorded against the Host's account. Repeated cancellations may lead to enforcement under Section 8.

No separate cancellation fee is charged. Where the Host relies on a Force Majeure Event, the Host must notify OpenBookings in writing as soon as reasonably practicable and provide reasonable supporting evidence. OpenBookings will determine in good faith whether the circumstances qualify.

### 3.5 No-Shows

Where a Guest fails to arrive for a confirmed Booking (a No-Show):

- The Host may mark the Booking as a No-Show in the business portal once its check-in window on the arrival day has closed, and no later than twenty-four (24) hours after the booked check-out time;
- No amount is refunded to the Guest, including Tourist Tax (Annex B, B.5.1);
- The Host keeps the full amount paid, and the Commission on the Booking stands;
- A Booking marked as a No-Show cannot be reviewed by the Guest.

### 3.6 Overbooking & Relocation

The Host bears the full risk of overbooking. Where the Host is unable to accommodate a Guest holding a confirmed Booking:

- The Host must notify the Guest and OpenBookings without delay, and use its best endeavours to source alternative accommodation of equivalent or higher standard, at the Host's own cost and at no additional cost to the Guest;
- Any relocation, transport, or other costs, and any claims by the Guest, are for the Host's account;
- If the Booking is not honoured, it is a Host-initiated cancellation under Section 3.4, and the Guest receives a full refund of the total price paid.

OpenBookings has no obligation to arrange or pay for alternative accommodation.

<Section id="payments" number="4" title="Payments, Commission and Taxes">

### 4.1 Payments

The Guest pays the Host directly. The accommodation contract is entered into directly between the Guest and the Host, and the Host is the merchant of record for all accommodation services provided. OpenBookings takes Bookings and applies the Host's Cancellation Policy on the Host's behalf, but is not a party to the accommodation contract.

Each Booking is paid in full at the time of booking, through a Stripe direct charge on the Host's Stripe Account. The Host acknowledges that:

- OpenBookings never receives, holds, or controls Guest payments. Funds are paid into the Host's Stripe Account and are subject to the Host's own agreement with Stripe;
- Payouts from the Host's Stripe Account to the Host's bank account follow the payout schedule the Host sets in its own Stripe Dashboard. OpenBookings does not initiate, delay, or withhold payouts;
- Stripe charges its payment processing fees, and any dispute fees, directly to the Host under the Host's agreement with Stripe. These fees are not OpenBookings' revenue, and OpenBookings adds no markup to them;
- The Host must maintain a connected and verified Stripe Account in good standing.

Pay-at-Property Extras are the only part of a stay that is not paid through the Platform. Their price is calculated and shown to the Guest at booking, and the Guest pays it to the Host at the property. The Host must not charge more than the price shown.

OpenBookings charges Guests no booking or service fees.

### 4.2 Commission

The Host pays OpenBookings a Commission on each Booking. The Commission is **4.5% of the Accommodation Price**, unless a different rate for a property has been agreed with OpenBookings in writing.

- The Commission is never charged on Tourist Tax or on Pay-at-Property Extras;
- The Commission is calculated per Booking and rounded to the nearest cent (half a cent rounds up);
- The Commission is taken automatically as a Stripe application fee at the moment the Guest pays;
- The Commission rate and amount are fixed on each Booking when it is made. A change of rate never applies to existing Bookings.

The Commission includes any VAT due on it (see Section 4.4). OpenBookings may change the Commission rate by giving at least thirty (30) days' written notice in accordance with Section 11.2.

### 4.3 Commission on Refunds and Disputes

When a Booking is refunded in whole or in part, OpenBookings returns its Commission in the same proportion as the refunded part of the Accommodation Price bears to the full Accommodation Price. For example, if the Guest gets 80% of the Accommodation Price back, OpenBookings returns 80% of its Commission. Tourist Tax is never part of this proportion: a refund of Tourist Tax alone returns no Commission, and Tourist Tax in a refund does not increase the Commission returned. A full refund of the Accommodation Price returns the full Commission. Refunds the Host makes outside OpenBookings, directly in its own Stripe Dashboard (Section 5.1), return no Commission. A dispute the Host loses (Section 5.3) does not show which part was Accommodation Price and which part was Tourist Tax; for a lost dispute, OpenBookings returns its Commission in the same proportion as the amount lost bears to the full amount the Guest paid, and never more than the full Commission. Stripe does not return its processing fee on a refunded payment; that fee remains with the Host.

### 4.4 Commission Invoices

OpenBookings issues one Commission Invoice per calendar month (Europe/Amsterdam time), covering the Commission on Bookings paid in that month and any Commission returned in that month. The Commission Invoice:

- Is issued after the month closes and by the 15th day of the following month at the latest, and is emailed to the billing email address in the Host's billing details;
- Is marked as paid, because the Commission was already taken at the moment of payment. Nothing further is payable;
- Includes 21% Dutch VAT in the Commission for Hosts established in the Netherlands. For Hosts established in another EU member state with a valid VAT number, VAT is reverse charged and the Host accounts for it in its own country;
- Comes with a specification of each Booking (reference, payment date, stay dates, Accommodation Price, Commission and Stripe payment), without Guest names;
- Becomes a credit note when the Commission returned in a month exceeds the Commission taken.

No Commission Invoice is issued for a month without Bookings or refunds.

### 4.5 Platform Records

The systems and records of OpenBookings (including the business portal, Commission Invoices, and email confirmations), together with the records of Stripe, shall constitute prima facie evidence of the existence and value of Bookings and the Commission due, unless the Host provides reasonable and credible counter-evidence. The Host must raise any objection to a Commission Invoice within thirty (30) days of receiving it.

### 4.6 Rates and Taxes

The Host is solely responsible for all taxes applicable to its accommodation services, including VAT, tourist taxes, and local levies, and for paying them to the competent authorities.

- **Rates include VAT.** The Host enters every rate including VAT and any mandatory fees, and confirms this on record before rates can be published. OpenBookings does not add VAT to the Host's rates.
- **Tourist Tax is added on top.** The Host sets up its tourist tax rules in the business portal. OpenBookings calculates the Tourist Tax from those rules, and adds it to the price shown to the Guest as a separate line. The Guest pays it into the Host's Stripe Account together with the Accommodation Price; OpenBookings never holds it. The Host is responsible for keeping its rules correct and for paying the Tourist Tax to the municipality.
- **DAC7.** OpenBookings is a platform operator under Council Directive (EU) 2021/514 (DAC7). The Host must provide the information OpenBookings needs to meet its reporting obligations, such as its tax identification number and registered address, and OpenBookings reports it to the Dutch Tax Administration as required.

### 4.7 Amounts Owed by the Host

Where the Host owes OpenBookings an amount under this Agreement that was not taken as an application fee, OpenBookings invoices it and the Host pays it within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. OpenBookings does not deduct amounts from Guest payments or from the Host's payouts.

<Section id="refunds" number="5" title="Refunds and Disputes">

### 5.1 Refunds

Every refund to a Guest is paid from the Host's Stripe Account to the Guest's original payment method, and is executed by OpenBookings on the Host's behalf through Stripe. OpenBookings never pays a refund from its own funds. A refund can arise in the following ways:

- **Under the Cancellation Policy.** Where Annex B requires a refund (for example a cancellation before the Cancellation Deadline, an early departure, or Tourist Tax for nights not stayed), OpenBookings executes it straight away, without further approval from the Host;
- **By the Host.** The Host may cancel a Booking (Section 3.4), record an early departure, or give a goodwill refund of any amount up to what is left on the Booking, through the business portal. The Host may always refund more than Annex B requires, never less;
- **By OpenBookings on defined grounds.** OpenBookings may issue a refund without the Host's approval where: (i) the booked room or a suitable equivalent is not available at check-in; (ii) the property is closed or not operating; (iii) the room or property differs materially from the Listing; or (iv) the Host's conduct breaches a Platform policy that has a defined refund remedy. OpenBookings will review each such claim, tell the Host the ground relied on, and refund only the affected nights where that is sufficient.

The Host should make refunds through the business portal, so that each refund shows which part is Accommodation Price and which part is Tourist Tax. Refunds the Host makes directly in its own Stripe Dashboard happen outside OpenBookings: they return no Commission (Section 4.3), OpenBookings records them, and OpenBookings may ask the Host to explain them. The Host remains responsible for the Tourist Tax on such refunds, including any correction with the municipality. If the balance of the Host's Stripe Account is insufficient for a refund, Stripe's rules for negative balances apply to the Host.

### 5.2 Stripe Fees on Refunds

Stripe does not return the original processing fee on a refunded payment. That fee is borne by the Host.

### 5.3 Disputes and Chargebacks

Because Guest payments are made into the Host's Stripe Account, a dispute or chargeback raised by a Guest's card issuer is raised against the Host's Stripe Account:

- Stripe notifies the Host and shows the dispute in the Host's Stripe Dashboard, with the deadline for a response;
- The Host is responsible for responding and submitting evidence (for example the Booking confirmation, the Cancellation Policy shown, and messages with the Guest) in its Stripe Dashboard. OpenBookings does not decide disputes and does not respond to them on the Host's behalf;
- If a dispute is lost, Stripe takes the disputed amount and its dispute fee from the Host's Stripe Account, and OpenBookings returns its Commission on the disputed amount in accordance with Section 4.3. The Host remains responsible for the Tourist Tax on any disputed amount.

On request, OpenBookings will provide the Host with the Booking records it holds that may help the Host respond.

<Section id="annex-b" title="Annex B: Cancellation Policy Tiers">

### B.1 Scope

**B.1.1** The Host selects one Cancellation Policy Tier in this Annex for the property: Flex or Standard (each a "Tier"). Every refundable Rate Plan follows that Tier. A Rate Plan may instead be offered as Non-refundable, subject to B.4. Custom, modified, or additional cancellation terms are not permitted.

**B.1.2** The Tier forms part of the contract between the Host and the Guest. OpenBookings applies the Tier on the Host's behalf, as set out in the Agreement.

**B.1.3** The Host may change the Tier, or whether a Rate Plan is Non-refundable, at any time. A change applies only to Bookings made after it takes effect. Every Booking remains subject to the Tier shown to the Guest at the moment of booking.

**B.1.4** The Host may always refund more than its Tier requires. The Host may never refund less.

### B.2 Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Check-in Time | The check-in time stated in the Booking confirmation, in the property's local time. If none is stated, 15:00 property local time on the arrival date. |
| Cancellation Deadline | The moment until which the Guest may cancel free of charge, as set by the Tier. |
| Cancellation Time | The moment the Guest cancels through the Platform, as recorded by the Platform. |
| Accommodation Price | The price of the booked nights, excluding Tourist Tax and Non-Delivered Services. |
| Tourist Tax | Any tourist or accommodation tax included in the price paid by the Guest. |
| Non-Delivered Services | Services included in the price that are not provided because the Guest does not stay (for example cleaning or breakfast). |
| Unused Night | A booked night after the Guest has left the property early. |

### B.3 The Tiers

| Tier | Cancellation Deadline | Cancelled before the deadline | Cancelled after the deadline |
|------|----------------------|-------------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Flex** | 24 hours before Check-in Time | Full refund | No refund of the Accommodation Price |
| **Standard** | 7 days before Check-in Time | Full refund | No refund of the Accommodation Price |
| **Non-refundable** | None | — | No refund of the Accommodation Price |

### B.4 Conditions for Non-refundable Rate Plans

**B.4.1** A Non-refundable Rate Plan may be offered on its own, or alongside Rate Plans on the Flex or Standard Tier for the same room type.

**B.4.2** Where, for the same room type and stay, the Host also offers a Rate Plan on the Flex or Standard Tier that is available for booking, the price of the Non-refundable Rate Plan for that stay must be at least 10% lower than the price of at least one such refundable Rate Plan.

**B.4.3** Where both are offered, OpenBookings displays the Non-refundable and refundable Rate Plans side by side. In every case, OpenBookings requires the Guest to select the Non-refundable Rate Plan explicitly before paying.

**B.4.4** OpenBookings may hide or suspend a Non-refundable Rate Plan that does not meet B.4.2 until it does.

### B.5 Rules that apply to every Booking

**B.5.1 No-shows.** If the Guest has not checked in and has not cancelled when the check-in window closes, no amount is refunded, including Tourist Tax and Non-Delivered Services.

**B.5.2 Early departure.** If the Guest leaves before the booked check-out date, the next 7 Unused Nights are not refunded. Any further Unused Nights are refunded in full. Unused Nights are counted from the night after the last night the Guest stayed, as recorded by the Host in the business portal.

**B.5.3 Tourist Tax and Non-Delivered Services.** On every Tier, Tourist Tax and Non-Delivered Services for nights the Guest does not stay are refunded in full, including after an early departure. This does not apply to a no-show (B.5.1).

**B.5.4 Cancellation by the Host.** If the Host cancels a Booking, the Guest receives a full refund of the total price paid, regardless of Tier. The consequences for the Host are set out in Section 3.4 of the Agreement.

**B.5.5 Property unable to provide the stay.** If the booked room or a suitable equivalent is not available, the property is closed or not operating, or the room or property differs materially from the listing, the Guest receives a full refund of the total price paid for the affected nights, regardless of Tier. This is in addition to the refund rights of OpenBookings in Section 5.1 of the Agreement.

**B.5.6 Travel restrictions and declared emergencies.** The Guest receives a full refund of the total price paid, regardless of Tier, if for the booked dates:

- **(a)** a government measure prohibits travel to, or a stay at, the place where the property is located, or prohibits entry into that country for residents of the Guest's country of residence; or
- **(b)** a competent authority has declared a disaster or emergency at that place that makes the stay impossible or unsafe.

The Guest must request this before the Check-in Time and provide reasonable evidence. A person at OpenBookings reviews each request. Personal circumstances, illness, and disruption of the Guest's own transport are not covered by this clause.

**B.5.7 Commission and fees.** On any refund, OpenBookings returns its Commission in proportion to the part of the Accommodation Price (as defined in Section 1.2 of the Agreement) that is refunded, as set out in Section 4.3 of the Agreement. Tourist Tax is never part of that proportion. Refunds made outside OpenBookings return no Commission, and lost disputes follow Section 4.3. Stripe's processing fee on the original payment is borne by the Host and is not returned.

**B.5.8 Execution.** Refunds required by this Annex are executed by OpenBookings on the Host's behalf from the Host's Stripe Account, without further approval from the Host. Amounts are rounded to the cent in the Guest's favour.

**B.5.9 Display.** Before booking and in the Booking confirmation, OpenBookings shows the Guest the Tier and the Cancellation Deadline as an exact date and time in the property's local time. The Host must not communicate cancellation terms to Guests that conflict with this Annex.
